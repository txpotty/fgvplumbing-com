    Sewage in a tub or floor drain means the main is blocked. Stop using water first. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Cape Girardeau

    Properties with chronic drain issues do better on a jetting schedule. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Root-prone home sewers usually jet every year or two, with camera records to track regrowth.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Cape Girardeau

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers need drains that keep up with heavy use every day. A backup closes rooms or stores. We schedule around slow hours and keep video records for property managers.

    Main Line Jetting in Cape Girardeau, MO

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. A working sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any home with a finished basement. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Cape Girardeau

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.
