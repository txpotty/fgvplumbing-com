    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Cape Girardeau

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Sudden downpours often overwhelm older sewer systems, which can push water back up through basement floor drains. Battery backup is an option.
