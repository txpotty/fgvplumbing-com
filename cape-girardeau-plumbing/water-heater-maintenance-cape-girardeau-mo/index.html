    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Cape Girardeau

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Cape Girardeau, MO

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. Leaky valves get replaced. We check the expansion tank too.

    Tankless Descaling in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and appliances. A pump runs descaler through the exchanger for the recommended time, followed by a clean water flush. We clean the inlet filter. We also check the burner and venting.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. We flush it with descaler. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.
