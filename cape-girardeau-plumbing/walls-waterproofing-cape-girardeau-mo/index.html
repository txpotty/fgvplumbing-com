    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Cape Girardeau

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Limestone and rocky ground in the southern part of the state can make digging for sewer lines harder and more costly. Backfill goes in last.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Hot, humid summers and cold winters give Missouri plumbing a wide range of stress, with big temperature swings in between. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Cape Girardeau

    Mid-century homes typically have copper supply lines, while newer houses rely on PEX and plastic drain pipe. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Cape Girardeau, MO

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Cape Girardeau

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Cape Girardeau take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. A wet electrical room can shut a business down.     Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.

    Sprinklers, nine times out of ten. Spray heads and drip lines aimed at the house soak the block every day, and soil sloped toward the wall or a short downspout adds to it, so the masonry stays wet and leaves mineral deposits as it dries. Sometimes adjusting a single sprinkler head solves it.

    Damp proofing is usually a thin spray-on asphalt coat. It slows soil moisture but will not stop water that is pushing against the wall under pressure. Waterproofing uses thicker membranes, drainage boards and footing drains rated to stop liquid water, which is what a basement in wet soil or a high water table needs.

    Every install gets a written warranty on materials and workmanship. Your proposal lists exactly what is covered and for how long, and when a manufacturer offers its own warranty on a membrane or resin we register it in your name. Store the paperwork with your home documents.

    Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard and Laticrete Hydro Ban cover most of our showers.     It depends on the wall and the site.
