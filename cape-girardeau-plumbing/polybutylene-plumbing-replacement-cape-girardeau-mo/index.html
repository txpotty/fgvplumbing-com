    Why Polybutylene Fails in Cape Girardeau

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Groundwater from limestone areas is especially mineral-heavy, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Cape Girardeau, MO

Heat tape and insulation on exposed runs get inspected and replaced where needed, so the new supply lines are protected under the home.
