    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Replacing an old tankless unit is usually simpler than a first-time conversion.     Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. Venting changes too. Both are on the estimate.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Cape Girardeau

    Commercial hot water loads in Cape Girardeau are heavier and more constant than residential. A restaurant can run a dish machine, hand sinks and a mop sink together, and the rack must hold temperature through the rush. Units share a header manifold. Service one, keep the others going.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Cape Girardeau, MO homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. Panel capacity gets checked up front.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Cape Girardeau

    Gas tankless units in Cape Girardeau are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. Gas is the catch. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. Their condensing and non-condensing lines both fit different homes, depending on the vent route and the budget you are working with. Non-condensing units vent in stainless. Condensing units vent in PVC and drain condensate.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Cape Girardeau

    Conversions in Cape Girardeau usually take a full day. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, the gas line is upsized and tested, a new vent goes out through a wall or roof, and the unit is mounted, plumbed with isolation valves and fired. You shower that night. The inspector comes after.

    Businesses in Cape Girardeau, MO with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. One unit can fail and the rest still carry the load. We size the rack by peak demand. Commercial installs usually need a larger gas service and a mechanical permit.
