    Clogged Drain Repair in Cape Girardeau

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Repeat clogs are a warning.     Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Drain seals wear out.     Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Poor original work causes some. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then cut out the damaged length and install new PVC or cast iron with transition couplings sized to both pipes. We test it before closing up.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. They clog the most. We clear the branch, replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and correct trap heights so the arm has the right fall to the wall. The disposal and dishwasher hookups get a look too.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in Cape Girardeau

    Downspout lines and yard drains fail without much warning. Water pools at the house. We clear the line, camera it for crushed or disconnected sections, and repair broken runs so downspout water gets carried away from the footing. We check the grade at the outlet too.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Every repair is done to code.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Cape Girardeau, MO

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. Expansive clay soils are common in Missouri, and they swell and shrink with moisture, putting stress on foundations and buried lines. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. We confirm it drains to daylight or a sump.
