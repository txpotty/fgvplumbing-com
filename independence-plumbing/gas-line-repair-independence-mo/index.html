    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Independence

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Expansive clay soils are common in Missouri, and they swell and shrink with moisture, putting stress on foundations and buried lines. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Independence, MO

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Independence, MO

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Independence, MO

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. Utilities get marked first. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Independence

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. Usually under an hour per appliance. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Independence, MO

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Uncoated brass connectors from decades ago crack at the flare fittings and should be replaced on sight. New coated connectors go in. We also add a reachable shutoff and a drip leg where needed, and confirm the new connector is rated for that appliance's BTU input.
