    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Independence, MO

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Independence, MO

    A single bad section does not always mean a repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. That works for one leak. By the third leak, though, we will talk to you honestly about whether the rest of that copper is close behind.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Independence

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Independence, MO

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Drywall patching may be separate. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.
