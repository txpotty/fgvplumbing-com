    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Independence

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. Two small pits, usually. We can pull new PEX or HDPE through the old pipe path or line a sewer lateral with resin, which leaves driveways, patios and mature landscaping in place. Not every pipe qualifies. The route and condition decide it.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Independence

    Sprinkler supply lines sit shallow and break easily. Landscapers hit them a lot. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in the growing season, and we fix the main, the backflow connection and the feed to the valves. We can reroute lines away from trees.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Independence, MO

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Underground Storm Drain Repair in Independence, MO

    Buried storm drains clog with silt, leaves and roots. Sudden downpours often overwhelm older sewer systems, which can push water back up through basement floor drains. Water backs up fast. We clear blockages with a jetter, find cracks and disconnected joints on camera, and replace damaged sections so runoff gets carried away from the house instead of soaking the foundation. Downspout tie-ins get checked.

    Storm Drain and Lateral Repair in Independence, MO

    Storm laterals carry roof and yard water away from the foundation. When they fail, water pools. We jet the line, camera it for crushed or separated sections and repair broken runs so runoff gets carried off instead of sitting next to the house. We check the discharge end.

    Foundation Leak Detection and Repair in Independence, MO

    A leak under a slab often shows up as a warm spot on the floor. Sometimes you hear water running. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging, then repair it through a small slab cut or reroute the line overhead so the floor stays intact. We explain the tradeoffs.

    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in Independence

    Digging a trench across a mature yard is expensive to restore. Trenchless avoids most of it. Faster, too.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Independence

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene cracks without warning. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, which is why we recommend a full swap to PEX or copper threaded along the existing route. You will notice the pressure.
