    What Goes Into a Tankless Conversion in Independence, MO

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Independence

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. That narrows the model list fast.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Independence, MO

    Replacing an old tankless unit tends to go faster than a conversion. The gas line is already sized and the vent path already exists, so in many homes it is a matter of matching the connections, checking the vent for damage and hanging the new unit. Different brands vent differently. We confirm fit first.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Independence, MO

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and replacement parts are widely stocked. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. Non-condensing units vent in stainless. Condensing units vent in PVC and drain condensate.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Independence

    Propane homes in Independence are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Since propane runs at different pressure from natural gas, the regulator, line sizing and gas valve must all match, and the supplier may need to swap the tank regulator. A small tank can run short.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Independence, MO

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Independence, MO homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. We check the panel first.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Independence

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Navien vents in PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Independence, MO

    Upfront, a tankless install in Independence, MO costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. The gap shrinks over time. Lower standby loss, a lifespan of up to 20 years against about 10 for a tank, and parts that can be replaced instead of the whole unit all count toward the total cost of ownership. We price both side by side.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Independence

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.
