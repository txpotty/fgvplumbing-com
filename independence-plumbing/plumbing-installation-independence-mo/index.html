    Water Softener Installation in Independence, MO

    Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. A hardness test at the kitchen tap, in grains per gallon, tells us whether a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner fits your usage. The bypass valve is not optional. With it, the softener can be serviced while the house still has water.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Installs in Independence

    Bradford White and Rheem both make good tanks, and the brand matters less than the fit. Demand, available space and the cost of gas versus electric at your address decide the model we recommend. Then we check the Missouri code items. On a closed system with a check valve or pressure reducing valve, an expansion tank is usually required, and we set it with the heater.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Independence, MO

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. CSST must be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Backflow Preventer Installation in Independence

    Irrigation systems and fire sprinkler lines are the usual reasons a home needs a backflow preventer. It keeps fertilizer-laced lawn water from being pulled back into the drinking supply when a main break or hydrant flush drops pressure. Type matters. We pick the assembly for the hazard level and local requirement and test it on install day.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Independence

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. Sizing comes first. The pump gets matched to the fill rate, wired to a dedicated circuit and piped out through a check valve.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Independence, MO

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main. We size the pump and basin to the fixtures it serves, vent the basin to code, and put a check valve and a full-port shutoff on the discharge so the pump can be pulled for service. We usually install Liberty or Zoeller pumps.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Independence

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. A PEX repipe ends that cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. Access holes are kept small and patched, or left square and clean for your drywall contractor.
