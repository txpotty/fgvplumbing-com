    Effluent Pump Replacement in Independence, MO

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor.     Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Independence, MO

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Repair for Businesses in Independence, MO

    Daycares and small shops on septic put more flow through the tank, more often, than a typical three-bedroom house with four people in it.
