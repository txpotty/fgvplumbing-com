    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Independence, MO

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. A flushometer that runs, a urinal that will not stop, or a sensor faucet that drips are usually fixed with a diaphragm kit, a solenoid or a new battery pack. We stock those. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Independence

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by.     Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Independence, MO

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. We set up a flushing and descaling schedule, and we install thermostatic mixing valves so hand sinks deliver tempered water without scalding anyone.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Independence

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Independence

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Basements are common in many areas, so sump pumps and foundation drainage are an important part of keeping homes dry. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.
