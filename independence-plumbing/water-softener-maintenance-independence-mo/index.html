    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Independence, MO

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.

    Softener Maintenance Plans and Pricing in Independence, MO

    The plan is one flat yearly fee. It covers the service visit and both hardness tests, and parts installed on a plan visit come at a reduced price. Catching one stuck float or clogged injector early usually covers the fee.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Independence

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle tend to mean the seal and spacer stack is worn out. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a piston and seal kit swap, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. It costs a fraction of a new control head.

    Commercial Softener Service in Independence

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so a twin-tank system keeps one tank online while the other recharges. We set a service schedule around your hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Resin Rebedding in Independence

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Independence

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. Raw water gets tested from an unsoftened hose bib or with the valve on bypass. Softened water comes from the kitchen tap.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Independence

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. The inlet and outlet piping, bypass, drain line and brine overflow all get traced and checked. Green crust on copper fittings, a drip at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Independence

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. Figure on an hour for a typical unit.
