    French Drain Installation in Independence

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.

    Sink, Tub and Toilet Drain Clearing in Independence

    Kitchen sinks clogged with grease and bathroom drains slowed by hair make up most of our fixture calls. We pull the trap when we can. Toilets get a closet auger, and if that fails, we pull the toilet and clear it from the flange.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Independence, MO

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send runoff straight into a garage or a door threshold. A channel drain catches that sheet flow.
