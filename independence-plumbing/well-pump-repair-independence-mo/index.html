    Well Pump Problems We Fix in Independence, MO

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Independence

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. FGV Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Independence, MO well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Independence, MO

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. Breaker trips usually come first. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets Missouri code when the job is done.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Independence

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Emergency Well Pump Repair in Independence

    No water at all means a house that cannot run. FGV Plumbing handles emergency well pump calls in Independence at the top of the schedule. The technician arrives with a pressure gauge, an amp clamp and the common switches, capacitors and relays, which covers a good share of no-water calls on the first trip. A full pump swap takes longer.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Independence

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Independence, MO

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.

    Well Pump Maintenance & Repair Cost in Independence, MO

    Maintenance costs far less than an emergency pull. A typical well pump repair in Independence runs $400 to $1,500, while a yearly checkup costs a fraction of that. FGV Plumbing prices both up front. We check tank precharge, switch contacts, amp draw and the well cap, which catches most problems early.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Independence, MO

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. A working sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any home with a finished basement. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. Arrestors are cheap.
