    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Independence, MO

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats hang up once minerals coat the rod. In tight pits where nothing has room to swing, we can switch you to an electronic sensor switch with no moving float at all.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Independence

    A working sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any home with a finished basement. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. That leaves time to replace a weak pump before the first big storm, not during it.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Independence, MO

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Independence

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. Sooner or later the pump sucks it into the screen and locks up the impeller. We pump the basin dry, rinse the screen and check the plastic liner for cracks, since a cracked liner lets soil wash in from the sides. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Independence

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. We pump the basin down and scoop out the sediment by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Independence

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Independence

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. We match the float and seals to the model number on the label. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne get two different checklists. We know both.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Independence

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.
