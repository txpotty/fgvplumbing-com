    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Springfield, MO: Style by Style

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Springfield, MO

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. The disposal gets mounted, the dishwasher drain goes through an air gap or high loop, and the dishwasher gets its own hot line and shutoff. One visit costs less than three. Every connection is tested under real use.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Springfield, MO

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. Power is a separate question. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Springfield

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Springfield

    Cheap stainless bowls are noisy when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before it goes in. It is a small thing. After the sink is in place the underside is hard to get at, which is why we do it while the bowl is still sitting out.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Springfield

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Springfield

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Springfield, MO

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.
