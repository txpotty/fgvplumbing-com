    Sump Pump Types We Install in Springfield, MO

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Springfield, MO

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Look at the breaker and the GFCI outlet, then pull the lid and check the float for a hang-up. A shop vac or a bucket can hold the level down. Our trucks carry common sizes, so most emergency calls end with a new pump running the same visit.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Springfield, MO

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. We route the outlet away from the house. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Springfield

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Sometimes a new switch is all you need, and sometimes the motor windings are shot. You will know which before we quote a pump.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Springfield

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Springfield, MO

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. Narrow pits, under about 12 inches across, are where they make the most sense.

    Wayne Sump Pump Swaps in Springfield

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. We replace it with the right Wayne or another cast iron unit. Figure on a couple of hours.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Springfield

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. New valve every time. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Springfield, MO

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.
