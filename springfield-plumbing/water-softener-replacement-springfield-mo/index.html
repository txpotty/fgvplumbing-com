    Picking the Right Softener for Springfield, MO Water

    Softener Loop Installation in Springfield, MO

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Springfield, MO

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Springfield, MO

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. We set it before we leave.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Springfield

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Springfield

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. You will buy less salt. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Springfield

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Springfield, MO

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Springfield

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Nearby Water Softener Replacement in Springfield, MO

    A local plumber can also check how the softener ties into your water heater and main line. We spot loop leaks and drain problems that cut performance, like a drain line with no air gap or a bypass valve that was left half open. Quotes are straightforward.

    Local water varies. City water and well water a few miles apart can test very differently, and iron or chlorine changes the setup. The system matches your test, not a guess.
