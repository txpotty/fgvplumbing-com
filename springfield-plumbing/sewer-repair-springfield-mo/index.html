    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Springfield

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Springfield

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups.     Sewer Camera Inspection in Springfield

    Every repair starts with a look inside. The camera goes in at the nearest cleanout.     Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Springfield

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Springfield, MO

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Limestone and rocky ground in the southern part of the state can make digging for sewer lines slower and more costly. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Springfield, MO

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and show you whether a liner or a spot repair would stop the cycle for good. Mature trees in Springfield yards make this a common call.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Springfield

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then run water through it and check it on camera before backfill. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Springfield

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.
