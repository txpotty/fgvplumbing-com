    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Usually a quick fix.

    Buried Line Repair in Springfield

    Buried pipes can be damaged by settling, roots or deep frost. Hard freezes happen every winter in Missouri, and the northern part of the state usually sees longer cold spells than the south.     Galvanized Pipe Repair in Springfield

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. Repairs are possible.
