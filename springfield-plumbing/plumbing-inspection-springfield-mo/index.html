    Plumbing Inspection Work in Springfield, MO

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Springfield

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. Expansion tanks, drain pans and softeners get a look as well, and a quick hardness test tells us whether the softener is actually doing its job or just using salt.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Springfield, MO

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Springfield, MO

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.

    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Springfield

    Businesses in Springfield use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns. We look at backflow assemblies, water heaters, restroom flush valves, floor drains and trap primers and the building sewer. Closures are expensive. With a ranked report, repairs get scheduled after close instead of forcing a shutdown.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Springfield

    Homes around Springfield on private wells should test for hardness and bacteria before picking a filter. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Springfield

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Springfield, MO

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.
