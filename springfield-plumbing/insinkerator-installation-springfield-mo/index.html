    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Springfield

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.

    Badger Disposal Install in Springfield

    Most replacement disposals in local kitchens are a Badger. A 1/3 to 3/4 HP Badger handles normal scraps and fits almost any sink base cabinet. The unit hangs on its quick-lock mount, the discharge tube goes on, a cord kit gets added if needed and we finish with a cold-water test grind. About an hour, usually.

    Commercial Disposer Installation in Springfield, MO

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. A commercial disposer runs 1 to 5 HP on its own circuit, and many come with a wall control panel and a water solenoid. We mount the unit and plumb the water feed, and your electrician handles the circuit. Expect an inspector to check it. Local code may also limit where a disposer can discharge relative to the grease interceptor, so we confirm that before the job starts.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Springfield

    If your current disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips its reset button every week, replacement usually beats repair. Water from the bottom of the housing means the inner seal failed.     Evolution Upgrade Installs in Springfield, MO

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot.
