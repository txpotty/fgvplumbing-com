    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly.     Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Springfield

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Springfield

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Springfield, MO

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Springfield, MO

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.
