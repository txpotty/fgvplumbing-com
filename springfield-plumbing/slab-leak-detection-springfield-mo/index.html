    How We Find Slab Leaks in Springfield, MO

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Springfield

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or recommend a reroute that never touches the concrete.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Springfield, MO

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. It only works on hot lines. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Non-Invasive Slab Leak Locating in Springfield, MO

    We touch the floor last. Pressure tests, line tracing, thermal scans and acoustic listening all happen from the surface in your Springfield, MO home, without drilling or cutting anything. Only after two methods agree do we mark the spot and talk about repair options, including ones that skip the concrete entirely.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Springfield

    The hot side fails more than the cold. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Gas or electric bills may jump too.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Springfield

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Residential Slab Leak Detection in Springfield

    Single-family homes and townhouses across Springfield get the same step-by-step process. Meter test first. We isolate, trace and listen, then confirm with a thermal scan or tracer gas, then explain what we found and give you written repair prices.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Springfield, MO

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Springfield

    Water bubbling up through a tile joint or a sudden pressure drop tells you the leak got bigger. Shut the main. Our techs in Springfield respond quickly, isolate the leaking side so you can get water back on the rest of the house overnight, then return to locate and repair it properly.
