    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Springfield, MO

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Springfield, MO

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper pinholes. Copper wears through at rub points or from water chemistry, and PEX usually leaks at a poorly crimped fitting or where a rodent got to it. Trucks in Springfield, MO stock fittings for each type.
