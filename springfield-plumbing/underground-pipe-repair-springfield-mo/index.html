    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in Springfield

    Digging a trench across a mature yard is expensive to restore. Pull-through methods save the lawn.     Foundation Leak Detection and Repair in Springfield, MO

    A leak under a slab often shows up as a warm spot on the floor. Or a hissing sound.     Buried Sewer Lateral Repair in Springfield, MO

    Buried laterals crack from roots, settling and age. Video locates the break. We excavate at the mark, cut out the damaged length and replace it with Schedule 40 PVC, using shielded transition couplings that match the old and new pipe. Fall gets checked before we close it.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Springfield, MO

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.
