    Ball Faucet Repair in Jefferson City, MO

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Jefferson City

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. It happens faster with hard water. We clean the valve body when the old part comes out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Jefferson City

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Close the angle stops under the sink. Should those stops be stuck, the house main shutoff is the backup. We come fast for these.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Jefferson City, MO

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Both are quick fixes. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Jefferson City, MO

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Jefferson City

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Jefferson City, MO

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. Spouts install two ways. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Jefferson City
