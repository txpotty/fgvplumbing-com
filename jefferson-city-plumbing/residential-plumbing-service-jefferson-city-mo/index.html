    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Jefferson City

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Basements are common in many areas, so sump pumps and foundation drainage are an important part of keeping homes dry.     Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Jefferson City, MO

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Older brick homes in Missouri often still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains, which corrode and narrow over time. Repipes are done in PEX or Type L copper, running new lines through the attic and walls with small access holes, and the water stays on at night for most of the job. A typical repipe takes two to four days.

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Jefferson City

    On a slab foundation, a supply leak can run for weeks unseen. Warm spots on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off or a water bill that doubles are the usual clues.     Repiping and Supply Line Replacement in Jefferson City

    Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning. We patch the access holes, ready for paint.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Jefferson City, MO

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. We test static pressure with a gauge on a hose bib, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. Seized main shutoffs get replaced too.
