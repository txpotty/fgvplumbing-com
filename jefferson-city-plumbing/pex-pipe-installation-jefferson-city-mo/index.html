    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Jefferson City

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, usually 18 to 36 inches down. Expansive clay soils are common in Missouri, and they swell and shrink with moisture, putting stress on foundations and buried lines. It gets bedded in sand, not rocky backfill.     PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Jefferson City, MO

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.
