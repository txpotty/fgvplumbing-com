    How We Install Showers in Jefferson City, MO

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Jefferson City, MO

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. A cracked pan or a leaking valve means pulling the shower, checking the framing and getting a new unit in as soon as it is safe. The framing must be dry. Then the new shower goes in.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Jefferson City, MO

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Jefferson City

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Steel and acrylic tubs need a mortar bed or foam under them, and cast iron needs a level ledger, so the tub sits flat and the caulk joint at the wall does not crack. The valve goes in next. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Jefferson City, MO

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. Slab homes need concrete work. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Jefferson City

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Testing follows. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Jefferson City, MO

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. The valve is where to spend. You get the full price before we start.

    Shower Pan Installation in Jefferson City

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. We level prefab pans carefully. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Jefferson City, MO

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.
