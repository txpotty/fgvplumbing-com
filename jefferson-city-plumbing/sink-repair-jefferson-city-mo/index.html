    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Jefferson City

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Jefferson City

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated garages are the usual trouble spots when temperatures drop sharply. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Emergency Sink Repair in Jefferson City, MO

    Shut the angle stops first. Turn both oval handles under the sink clockwise until they stop, and if they spin freely or keep weeping, go to the main shutoff and close it before calling us. We send active leaks to the front of the line. The plumber brings stops and supply lines to stop it on the first visit.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Jefferson City, MO

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Jefferson City

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Jefferson City, MO

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Jefferson City, MO

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Jefferson City

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down all day, and nobody checks the trap until water is on the carpet. We clear it and add a strainer. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.
