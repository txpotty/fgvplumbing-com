    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Jefferson City

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead.     Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Jefferson City

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. The run from the sinks to the trap is where grease cools and builds up. Jetting a belly buys time, but only regrading the pipe fixes it.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Jefferson City

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. A cracked or loose baffle turns the trap into a pass-through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. Plastic units get a factory replacement part. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Jefferson City, MO

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Steel trap bodies also rust through at the bottom seams after enough years of hot, acidic water. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Sometimes it is a $15 gasket. An opened seam is another story, and we will say up front if a patch will not hold.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Jefferson City

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. The fix starts with pumping the trap down and jetting the inlet line until it flows again. Solids packed against the baffle come out by hand, with a scoop and a bucket. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.

You get repair and replacement priced side by side, in writing.
