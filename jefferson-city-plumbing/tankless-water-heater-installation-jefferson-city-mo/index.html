    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Jefferson City, MO

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Homes around Jefferson City on private wells should test for hardness and bacteria before picking a treatment system. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.
