    Running Toilet Fixes in Jefferson City

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper.     Toilet Brands We Service in Jefferson City

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield make up most of the toilets we open, and each one uses its own flapper size or flush valve design. The wrong flapper will leak. We match the part to the model number stamped inside the tank and stock the common ones on every truck.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Jefferson City

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run.     Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Jefferson City

    Hairline cracks in a tank can sit for years and then split open when someone sets a heavy lid down or a bolt is tightened too far.     Commercial Toilet Repair in Jefferson City

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Jefferson City, MO

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. Hardened rubber stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. When one tank seal has failed, we replace the full set so the others do not follow next month.

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Jefferson City, MO

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. A cracked handle arm or a stripped mounting nut takes about five minutes to replace.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Jefferson City, MO
