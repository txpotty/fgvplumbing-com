    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Jefferson City, MO

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. The pipe gets brittle from the inside, and fittings crack, so a home that has had one polybutylene leak is usually at risk everywhere else in the system. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Many insurers ask about it.

    Copper Repiping in Jefferson City, MO

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Expect a slightly longer job. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Jefferson City

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Limestone and rocky ground in the southern part of the state can make digging for sewer lines slower and more costly. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Jefferson City, MO

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Jefferson City, MO

Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.
