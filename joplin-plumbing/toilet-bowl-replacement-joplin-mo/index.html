    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Joplin

    Cracked Toilet Bowl Replacement in Joplin

    Most bowl cracks start near the bolt holes or at the base where the porcelain meets the floor, and a crack below the waterline leaks whether you can see it or not.
