    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes.     Leaking Disposal Fix in Joplin, MO

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. Side leaks are usually the discharge gasket or the dishwasher hose clamp.

    Emergency Disposal Repair in Joplin, MO

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits.
