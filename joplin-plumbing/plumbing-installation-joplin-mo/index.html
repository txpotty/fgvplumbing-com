    PEX Pipe Installation in Joplin, MO

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. In a repipe, fewer fittings means fewer hidden joints and fewer chances for a leak to soak a wall. It can also stretch when water freezes. Disconnecting hoses, insulating exposed lines and letting faucets drip on bitter nights prevent most freeze damage. Stretch or not, we still insulate runs in exterior walls and crawl spaces.

    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Joplin

    Groundwater from limestone areas is especially hard, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations. Undersize it and it regenerates all the time, burning through salt. We set the drain line with an air gap. Programming follows, set to your hardness, and we run one test cycle.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Joplin

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. We size before we sell. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in Joplin

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. A certified tester checks each assembly on install day and the results go to the water provider. That starts the annual clock. Most jurisdictions want a retest every 12 months, and we can put you on a reminder list so it does not slip.

    Electric tankless heaters pull serious amperage, often 120 amps or more for a whole-house unit.
