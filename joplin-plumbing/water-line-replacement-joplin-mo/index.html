    Copper Service Line Installation in Joplin, MO

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. Plenty of copper mains have lasted 60 years. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off.     PEX Water Line Replacement in Joplin

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Joplin, MO

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, leaving you with two small holes instead of a long trench. Rock can stop it.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Joplin, MO

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, carrying the NSF 61 listing.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Joplin, MO

    Pipe pulling works like this. We dig a pit at each end of the old line. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, breaking up the old line if it has gone brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.
