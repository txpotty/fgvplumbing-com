    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Joplin, MO

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property.     Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Joplin

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole.     Grease Trap Installation in Joplin

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it.     Backflow Testing and Certification in Joplin

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. We tag every passing device. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Joplin

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Limestone and rocky ground in the southern part of the state can make digging for sewer lines harder and more costly. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.
