    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Joplin, MO

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Most Joplin, MO softener installs land between $1,500 and $4,000. That typically covers the unit itself and the labor to plumb it in, program it and test it, with any loop or drain work listed separately. Capacity affects price. We give written pricing up front.

    Control valves usually carry about 5 years, and tanks often 10 or more. We also warranty our installation work.

    Standard residential softener installs often need no separate permit. Major main line or drain changes can need one, and we let you know. We handle the paperwork.

    Softeners with Fleck and Clack control valves are what we install most. Our picks favor good performance and easy service.

    Salt use depends on hardness, water use and how efficient the softener is. A family of four with fairly hard water might use 40 to 80 pounds of salt per month, and a metered high-efficiency model uses less. Metering cuts salt use.
