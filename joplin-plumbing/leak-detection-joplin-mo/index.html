    Where We Find Leaks in Joplin, MO

    Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Joplin, MO

    A brown ring on the ceiling under a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. We figure out which. Thermal cameras show the cool wet area, a moisture meter confirms it and a borescope through a small hole lets us see the actual pipe without opening the whole ceiling.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Joplin

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell come up on most of our calls. All of them mean water. A thermal scan shows the wet patch, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we open one small access hole rather than following the stain around the room.

    Pool Leak Detection in Joplin

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Joplin, MO

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.

    Fixture and Appliance Leaks in Joplin, MO

    One running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons daily. Worn faucet cartridges, softener valves and water heater connectors add more. We dye test tanks, check flappers and fill valves, and inspect supply lines and water heater fittings, and most of these repairs take under an hour with parts from the truck. They are inexpensive repairs.

    Slab Leak Signs in Joplin, MO

    Warm floor spots, cracked tile and a meter that never stops are the common signs. So is the sound of water with everything off. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests, then trace and listen through the slab, and once it is located we go over spot repair, reroute and repipe options with prices for each.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Joplin

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Every tool covers a different case. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, while tracer gas catches silent leaks on PEX and PVC.
