    Water Heater Maintenance in Joplin

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and fixtures. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Flushing takes about an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts years longer.

    Sump Pump Testing in Joplin, MO

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. A working sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any home with a finished basement. We fill the pit to test the float, check the discharge line and confirm the check valve holds. Battery backups get load tested.

    Softener Upkeep in Joplin, MO

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. We check the bypass and drain line. We measure hardness after.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Joplin

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. We check the brine level, clean the injector screen, inspect the resin bed for fouling and confirm the regeneration cycle starts and finishes, then test the treated water hardness at a faucet. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Older resin may need replacing.

    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in Joplin, MO

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated garages are the usual trouble spots when temperatures drop sharply. During maintenance we look for exposed pipes in garages and crawl spaces, hose bibs without vacuum breakers and outdoor lines that need draining, and we fix those before the season that tests them. Pipe sleeves cost little. Water damage costs plenty.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Joplin

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Many water providers require yearly testing on irrigation and commercial backflow preventers, and our certified testers check each check valve and relief valve and file the results with your utility. We repair failures. Weather protection gets checked too. Late tests can mean fines.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Joplin

    Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, which are best replaced when other plumbing work is planned. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried lines until they leak out of sight for months. Warm floors are a clue. A spinning meter is another. Acoustic and thermal gear pinpoint it.
