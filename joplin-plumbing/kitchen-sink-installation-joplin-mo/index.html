    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Joplin, MO

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in.     Composite Sink Installation in Joplin

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Joplin

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Joplin, MO

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Venting happens at this stage. Having the sink and faucet specs early saves moving pipe later.
