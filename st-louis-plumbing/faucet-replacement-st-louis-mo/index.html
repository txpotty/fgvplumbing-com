    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in St. Louis

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. Touchless models need a dry spot on the cabinet wall for the control box and either batteries or an outlet within reach of the cord. Power gets checked first. Sensor range is set last.

    Corroded Faucet Replacement in St. Louis

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. A pitted body will not let a new cartridge seal, so the drip comes back a few weeks after each repair. At that point, replace it. We pull it, even with seized nuts, and set a new faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Crusty Bathroom Faucet Replacement in St. Louis, MO

    Crusty lav faucets are the most common swap we do. Out comes the old faucet, the deck gets scraped clean, and the new faucet goes in with a matching pop-up drain. It takes about an hour. The handles get set to close at the same angle.

    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in St. Louis

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. The pull-down hose wears where it bends over the spout neck, and once it splits, water runs down the inside of the faucet and into the cabinet without anyone seeing it. Sometimes a new hose is enough. When the body or cartridge is worn too, we swap the whole faucet and put new supplies on.

    Tub and Shower Trim Replacement in St. Louis, MO

    Tub and shower trim can be replaced without opening the wall. If your valve is a Moen Posi-Temp or a Delta Monitor, a trim kit from the same brand bolts right onto it and gives you new handles, a new spout and a new showerhead. The valve has to match. An outdated valve may need replacing instead.

    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Replacement in St. Louis, MO

    A split hose bib often looks fine from outside. Hard freezes happen every winter in Missouri, and the northern part of the state usually sees longer cold spells than the south. Most splits sit inside the wall, either from trapped water or plain wear on a valve that has been cranked hard for years. The wall behind it gets checked. Frost-free sillcocks with built-in vacuum breakers go in as the replacement where freezing is possible.

    Commercial Faucet Replacement in St. Louis

    Restaurants lose money every hour a hand sink is down. We replace commercial faucets after close or before open, and we bring the most common pre-rinse and hand sink models so the job finishes in one visit. Lever handles meet ADA rules. Hand sinks go first, since the health inspector checks them.

    A cracked faucet body cannot be patched. With water spraying and a stop that will not shut, go straight to the main valve by the meter or where the line comes in. Then give us a call. We send a plumber with standard faucets on the truck, so the replacement often happens on the same visit.
