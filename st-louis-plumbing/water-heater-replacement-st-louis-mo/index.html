    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis

    Businesses in St. Louis put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery.     Electric Water Heater Replacement in St. Louis

    Electric water heaters in St. Louis usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.
