    Sewer Line Replacement Methods in St. Louis, MO

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in St. Louis, MO

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Without one, the plumber has to pull a toilet or go through the roof vent. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, with a cap at ground level you can find, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in St. Louis, MO

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. A hairline crack is enough. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and cutting them out with a machine only buys a few months, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in St. Louis

    Digging is still the right method for a lot of lines. A collapsed pipe cannot guide a bursting head. Sharp bends, a belly that needs regrading, or a route that should change all point toward an open trench, and we leave the yard as close to how we found it as dirt allows. Grass grows back.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in St. Louis, MO

    Trenching lets us fix grade. Correcting slope is where it shines. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, while a trench lets us lay new pipe at the right pitch. Bellies disappear.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in St. Louis

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and only bury pipe rated for it. Paperwork stays on file.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in St. Louis

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. A single bad joint can usually be dug and fixed. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet tell us the line is failing everywhere, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in St. Louis

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. We dig with a mini excavator and hand dig near utility marks. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, confirmed with a level on every length. Then the camera.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in St. Louis

    Bigger pipe, more flow, more rules. Most commercial lines are 6-inch or larger. We handle the permit, set up bypass pumping when the building cannot shut down, and arrange traffic control when the work reaches the road, keeping the outage as short as the job allows. Night shifts possible.

    Sewer Line Replacement Near St. Louis, MO

    A local crew can come back for the inspection. On sewer jobs that is a big deal. The trench usually has to stay open until the inspector signs off, so we plan our schedule in St. Louis around the inspector's visit and keep the hole covered, fenced and marked overnight until it passes.
