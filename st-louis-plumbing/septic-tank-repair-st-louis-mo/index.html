    Drain Field Line Jetting in St. Louis

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in St. Louis

    Gurgling drains right after a pump-out point to the line between the house and the tank, not the tank itself. That section sags or breaks from roots and settling soil. A camera from the house cleanout finds the bad joint, and we mark it in the yard. Then we dig one hole, not a trench. A second cleanout near the tank goes in while it is open.

    After-Hours Septic Backup Calls in St. Louis

    Late-night backups usually start in the lowest drain in the house, often a basement floor drain or a first-floor shower.     Septic Line Clog Clearing in St. Louis

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter set up for a 4-inch building sewer. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and the fix after clearing depends on which one we pull out. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Drain Field Repair in St. Louis, MO

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload from a running toilet can do it too. We test before recommending anything big. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in St. Louis

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. Our tech confirms the pump runs and looks for a float snagged on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.
