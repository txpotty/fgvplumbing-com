    Vessel Sink Installation in St. Louis, MO

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in St. Louis

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. A same-size drop-in swap usually takes a morning, and a deeper bowl may only need a new trap. New counters add a day. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in St. Louis

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Utility Sink Installation in St. Louis

    Laundry and garage tubs take dirty water and a lot of it. The tub goes on a solid stand or bracket with a 1-1/2 inch trap, plus a faucet tall enough to fill a bucket. Groundwater from limestone areas is especially hard, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. We add a cleanout plug.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in St. Louis, MO

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Undermount Sink Installation in St. Louis

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in St. Louis

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. Each one needs its own support, and a cast iron farmhouse sink can weigh well over a hundred pounds before it holds any water, so the cabinet often needs reinforcement first. The cabinet gets checked before install day. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in St. Louis, MO

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.
