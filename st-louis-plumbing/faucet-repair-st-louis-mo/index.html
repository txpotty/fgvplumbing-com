    Hard Water Faucet Repair in St. Louis

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. We clean the valve body when the old part comes out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in St. Louis, MO

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. New O-rings go on and the mounting nut gets tightened, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in St. Louis, MO

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in St. Louis, MO

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in St. Louis, MO

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. Swapping a common cartridge like the Moen 1225 takes about half an hour, cleanup included.

    Disc Faucet Repair in St. Louis

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in St. Louis, MO

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. Commercial cartridges are on the truck. We set metering faucets to run long enough for a full rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in St. Louis

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.
