    Gym and Locker Room Showers in St. Louis

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. Commercial valves with metered or push-button controls keep run times in check, and the drains are sized for heavy use. Accessible stalls get handhelds on slide bars. We phase the work to keep some showers running.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in St. Louis

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in St. Louis

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Pressure varies by house. We test static pressure before designing the layout.

    Digital Shower Systems in St. Louis, MO

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. The outlet is an electrician's job. We plumb the valve and outlets.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in St. Louis

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. A new pan, a tub conversion or a new valve and trim can change the bathroom without a gut job. We price the options side by side. Most of these finish in a few days.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in St. Louis

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on.     Curbless Shower Installation in St. Louis

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.
