    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in St. Louis, MO

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in St. Louis, MO

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. A submersible pull and replacement runs about $1,800 to $5,000 and up, based on depth and horsepower. Replacing an aging pump during a pull saves paying for a second pull later. We lay out both options.

    Jet Pump System Repair in St. Louis, MO

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. FGV Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Pressure Tank Repair in St. Louis, MO

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.
