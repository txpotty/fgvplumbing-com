    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in St. Louis

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. Usually under an hour per appliance. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    CSST Repair in St. Louis

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. We check bonding on every CSST job. The system should be bonded to the electrical grounding system with a proper clamp and conductor, per the manufacturer's instructions and local code.

    Propane Gas Line Service in St. Louis, MO

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas problems do not wait for business hours, and losing heat or hot water overnight is a real problem. We answer late calls.     Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in St. Louis

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in St. Louis

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.
