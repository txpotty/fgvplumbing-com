    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in St. Louis

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean.     Leaking Grease Trap Repair in St. Louis, MO

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling.     Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in St. Louis, MO

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. We put both options in writing with a price on each.

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. Once a riser cracks, rainwater and grit wash straight into the tank.
