    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in St. Louis, MO

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room.     Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in St. Louis

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Disconnecting hoses, insulating exposed lines and letting faucets drip on bitter nights head off most freeze damage. Sun breaks down plastic traps and supply lines left outside, and yard dirt and grass clippings clog the drain faster than kitchen scraps ever do. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call.     Pop-Up Stopper Repair in St. Louis, MO

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in St. Louis, MO

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in St. Louis

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.
