    What a Bowl Swap Covers in St. Louis, MO

    Cracked Toilet Bowl Replacement in St. Louis

    Most bowl cracks start near the bolt holes or at the base where the porcelain meets the floor, and a crack below the waterline leaks whether you can see it or not. Some leak slowly, some all at once. We drain the tank, lift the tank off and remove the bowl, and the matching bowl goes in on a fresh seal with new tank gaskets and bolts.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in St. Louis

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. The bolt holes get a close look before we quote. A gasket and bolt kit fixes the first case, and a cracked bolt hole means a new bowl with stainless or brass hardware.

    Brand-Specific Bowl Replacement in St. Louis, MO

    Model numbers are stamped inside the tank, usually on the back wall, and sometimes on the back of the bowl near the floor. Send us a photo of it. Matched bowls come from local supply houses, and most arrive within a day or two unless the model has been discontinued.

    Flange Repair and Toilet Reset in St. Louis, MO

    A wobble at the base usually means the bowl has been rocking on a loose or broken flange, and every rock breaks the wax seal a little more. Old cast iron flanges tend to rust through. We repair the flange with a stainless ring, or replace it, before the new bowl goes down on a fresh seal.

    Replacing Stained Toilet Bowls in St. Louis, MO

    Groundwater from limestone areas is especially mineral-heavy, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. Iron and manganese in some water supplies leave orange or black stains that soak into worn glaze and will not scrub out once the surface is etched. Replacement is the only real fix. We recommend bowls with a fully glazed trapway, which stain far less and clean in seconds.

    Emergency Toilet Bowl Replacement in St. Louis, MO

    A bowl that splits open can dump the tank and every refill onto the floor until someone closes the angle stop. Close it first. Once the water is off, we remove the broken bowl in pieces if needed, wrap the shards and set a replacement or a full new toilet as fast as we can get one.

    Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in St. Louis

    Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated garages are the usual trouble spots when temperatures drop sharply. Water left sitting in a toilet in an unheated cabin or vacant house can freeze, and the ice cracks the bowl at the trap where you cannot see it. Toilets in unheated spaces should be drained. After replacing the bowl, we show you how to winterize it, which means shutting the stop, flushing and adding RV antifreeze to the bowl and tank.

    Chipped or Stained Bowl Replacement in St. Louis, MO

    Deep chips in the glaze collect bacteria, and hard water rings that have etched the surface will not come out with any cleaner. Replacing the bowl solves both problems. The bowl color gets matched to your tank, which is easy for white and harder for biscuit or bone fixtures that have faded over the years.
