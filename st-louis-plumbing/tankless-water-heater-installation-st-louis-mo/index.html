    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in St. Louis, MO

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and fixtures. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in St. Louis, MO

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. Venting changes too. We plan both on the quote.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in St. Louis, MO

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in St. Louis, MO

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in St. Louis, MO homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current.
