    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in St. Louis

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward.     Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in St. Louis, MO

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. We rebuild or replace the bypass and check the drain line for kinks. The drain line has to end above its receptor with an air gap.

    Resin Rebedding in St. Louis

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Repair or Replace a Softener in St. Louis, MO

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. Sound tanks and valves are worth keeping. We will tell you plainly to replace it when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in St. Louis, MO

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. A worn piston is a common find.

    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in St. Louis, MO

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. The top layer fuses into a crust with an empty pocket below it. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, so the resin never recharges and hard water flows to every tap.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in St. Louis, MO
