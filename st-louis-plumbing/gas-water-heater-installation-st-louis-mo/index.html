    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We size the heater from your peak use, then match the vent to the manufacturer table and code. Undersized vents spill CO. Draft gets tested on every job.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in St. Louis

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. A conversion often means a larger gas line, new venting, a condensate drain and service valves. It takes most of a day. We do all of it to code.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in St. Louis

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in St. Louis, MO

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.
