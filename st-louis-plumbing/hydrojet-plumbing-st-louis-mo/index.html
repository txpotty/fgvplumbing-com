    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in St. Louis

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in St. Louis, MO

    A full sewer backup needs a truck today. First, we open the line. A cable opens it, then the jetter removes the grease, roots or wipes that caused it.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in St. Louis, MO

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Low-slope lines clog more. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in St. Louis

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers need drains that keep up with heavy use every day. Downtime costs them money. We set up jetting plans for each building's needs, schedule work during slow hours, and keep the camera footage on file so property managers can show tenants or owners the condition of the lines.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in St. Louis

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in St. Louis

    Slow drains in several rooms usually mean buildup in a main or branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Most homes take two to three hours.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in St. Louis

    Backups at night or on weekends get the same equipment as weekday calls. On-call trucks are fully equipped. We can clear and jet a line in one visit instead of returning Monday.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in St. Louis

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.
