    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Oakville

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Oakville, MO

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. FGV Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Oakville, MO

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Oakville

    Older Oakville homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Commercial Trenchless Solutions in Oakville

    Commercial sites in Oakville have longer runs and more laterals. Pavement covers most of it.     Pipe Bursting Services in Oakville, MO

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Oakville, MO soil.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Oakville

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.
