    Septic Tank Fixes We Handle in Oakville, MO

    Septic Tee Repair in Oakville

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet can crack or get knocked off by the hose on a pump truck. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Oakville

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. We pump it down and look at the walls and seams with a light, and a dye test in the drains shows where water comes in or goes out. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    After-Hours Septic Backup Calls in Oakville

    Late-night backups usually start in the lowest drain in the house, often a basement floor drain or a first-floor shower. Our on-call tech carries a jetter and spare floats, which means most calls end with the drains working. Bad nights end with a pump truck. Either way, you know what failed before we leave.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Oakville, MO

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. We rebuild or replace linear and rotary aerators, plus clean out diffusers clogged with solids. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Septic Repair for Businesses in Oakville, MO

    Daycares and small shops on septic put more flow through the tank, more often, than a typical three-bedroom house with four people in it. Kitchen grease and restroom paper towels cause most of the trouble. Our fix often includes a bigger effluent filter and a pumping schedule tied to real use. Repairs get scheduled around your hours.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Oakville, MO

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age. Expansive clay soils are common in Missouri, and they swell and shrink with moisture, putting stress on foundations and buried lines. Roots and settling ground split joints and crush the pipe. We replace the bad run with 4-inch PVC, bedded in sand and sloped a quarter inch per foot. Short sections take an afternoon.

    Commercial Septic Repair in Oakville, MO

    Churches and small offices on septic run more water through the tank than any house, and grease from a kitchen makes it worse. We size repairs to the actual daily flow, not a residential guess. Grease interceptors and lift stations are part of it. Downtime costs you, so we schedule around open hours.
