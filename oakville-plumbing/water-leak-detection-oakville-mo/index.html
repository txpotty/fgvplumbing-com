    Irrigation Leak Detection in Oakville

    Main Service Line Leaks in Oakville, MO

    The service line carries full street pressure and may leak below ground for months. Soft spots in the lawn may be the only sign. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair, and we often recommend replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service pipe rather than patching it again.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Oakville

    The meter is the first and simplest leak test. Shut everything, then watch. If the leak indicator spins with every fixture and appliance off, water is escaping somewhere, and the flow reading tells us roughly how many gallons a day are being lost. Next, we close each zone in turn.

    Water Heater and Softener Leaks in Oakville

    A softener stuck in regeneration sends water down the drain nonstop. That shows up on the bill. Water heaters leak at fittings, drain valves and relief valves, while a dripping relief valve usually points to high pressure. We check both units on every visit.

    Smart Leak Detection Devices in Oakville

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn watch your water flow and can close the main on their own when they see a leak pattern. Sensors go by water heaters and washers. We install and set up the system so alerts come to your phone, and some insurers give a premium discount for having one.
