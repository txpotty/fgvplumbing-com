    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Oakville, MO

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Oakville

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. If the angle stop will not stop it, the main shutoff will, and the sooner it is closed the less water ends up inside the vanity floor and the subfloor below. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Oakville, MO

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Oakville, MO

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. A trap or stopper costs a fraction of a new sink, and replacement only makes sense when the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain body has rusted into the basin. We quote both ways if it is a close call. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Oakville

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Oakville

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Oakville, MO

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Oakville, MO

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.
