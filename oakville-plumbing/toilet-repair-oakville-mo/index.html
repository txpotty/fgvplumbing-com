    Running Toilet Fixes in Oakville

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper.     Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Oakville, MO

    The rubber parts in a toilet, the flapper, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the bolt washers and the wax ring at the floor, all break down with age and chlorine. They get stiff. A stiff gasket stops sealing and lets water seep out slowly where you will not see it until the floor stains. We replace the whole set of tank seals at once when one is gone.

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Oakville, MO

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties.     Clogged Toilet Clearing in Oakville, MO

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. At that point we run a cable from the nearest cleanout or send a camera down to see it.
