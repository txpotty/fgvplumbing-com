    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Oakville, MO

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. Each part gets tested. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Oakville, MO

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Hard water makes it worse, because the scale binds with soap scum and hair into a mat that sticks to the pipe wall and builds out over time. An auger cuts through it. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Oakville

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. The drain gets cleared and tested. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Oakville, MO

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. The source gets repaired. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Oakville, MO

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Oakville

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. A new cartridge fixes it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Oakville, MO

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Oakville, MO

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. A soak can bring it back. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.
