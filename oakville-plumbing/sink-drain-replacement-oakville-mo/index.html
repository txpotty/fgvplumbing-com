    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Oakville, MO

    Leaks right at the wall point to the trap arm or stub. A cracked arm or rusted stub drips down the back panel, out of sight, until the wood swells or smells. We cut back to solid pipe. Then new PVC gets tied in.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Oakville

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Over time that wears the thin plastic drain and trap on a utility sink until it cracks, and lint from the washer standpipe often builds up in the trap so badly that it never drains at full speed again. A 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout plug goes in. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Oakville, MO

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. They open and close with a press on the cap, and we check that the sink has an overflow before choosing a slotted body, since a slotted drain in a bowl without an overflow leaks out the slots. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Oakville, MO

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Choosing Drain Material in Oakville, MO

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It does not rust, it stands up to drain cleaners, and it costs less than brass, while polished 17-gauge brass makes sense under a pedestal sink or any trap you can see. Some houses use black ABS instead. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Oakville, MO

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Chemicals left sitting in a metal trap eat through it, so many old traps we pull have pinholes at the bottom. PVC does not have that problem. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Oakville

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common decades ago, and when we replace the drain we bring it up to a vented P-trap with the right trap arm length. An AAV may be allowed where a vent is missing. Local rules decide.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Oakville

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. If your laundry or utility sink sits below the sewer line, its drain runs to a sink pump or a sewage ejector, and we check the check valve and the discharge line when we replace the trap. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.
