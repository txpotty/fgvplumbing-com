    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Oakville

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Oakville

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Missouri houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers. Each gets tested, not guessed at. A corroded fitting or partial blockage found now will not become a ceiling stain or a holiday backup later.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Oakville, MO

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Oakville, MO

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Oakville, MO

    Older homes in Oakville often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. Our camera shows each defect, and the onboard counter tells us exactly how far from the cleanout it sits. Then we locate it. With depth and position marked, repair pricing gets accurate, and choosing between a spot fix, a liner or replacement gets easier.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Oakville

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Oakville, MO

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Oakville, MO

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.
