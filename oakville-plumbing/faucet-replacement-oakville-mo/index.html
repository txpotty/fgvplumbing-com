    Corrosion-Resistant Faucet Choices in Oakville, MO

    Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and fixtures. Finish and body material decide how a faucet ages. Solid brass bodies with PVD finishes resist pitting and hold their color, while plated zinc and thin chrome flake once minerals get under the coating. Brushed stainless hides water spots. You see the choices before you order.

    Moen Faucet Replacement in Oakville

    Moen's 1225 cartridge runs a huge share of single-handle Moen faucets. A stuck 1225 makes the handle stiff and the temperature wander, and after many years in place it can be cemented in by minerals. Sometimes pulling it cracks the body. At that point a new faucet is the fix.

    Commercial Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Oakville

    Commercial kitchens are hard on faucets. Pre-rinse units and hand sink faucets get swapped for T&S or Chicago Faucets models, both of which stock parts for a long time. We work around your hours. Every install meets health and accessibility code.

    Urgent Faucet Replacement in Oakville, MO

    Water that will not shut off at the faucet is an urgent call. Close the stop under the sink by turning it clockwise, and if the stop is seized or leaks at the stem, close the main. Urgent calls get priority. A plumber stops the flow, removes the broken faucet and installs a new one from the truck.

    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Oakville

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. Buckets hung on the spout and hoses yanked off the threads loosen the faucet and eventually crack its supply joints. We use brass-bodied replacements. The new faucet is anchored tight to the wall or the tub.

    Outdoor Spigot Replacement in Oakville, MO

    Outdoor spigots wear out from sun, hoses and hard closing. Disconnecting hoses, insulating exposed lines and letting faucets drip on bitter nights head off most freeze damage. Worn spigots get replaced, with frost-free models where needed and a vacuum breaker on each, which code calls for on every hose thread. The wall cavity gets a look too. Old leaks sometimes soak the insulation.

    Emergency Faucet Replacement in Oakville, MO

    A cracked faucet body cannot be patched. If water is spraying from the base or the handle and the stop under the sink will not close, shut the main valve where the line enters the house. Then call us. Our trucks carry standard faucets, which means the swap is often done on that first visit.

    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Oakville

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. Touchless models need a dry spot on the cabinet wall for the control box and either batteries or an outlet within reach of the cord. We check that first. We adjust the sensor range at the end.
