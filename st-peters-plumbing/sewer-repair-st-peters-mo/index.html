    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in St. Peters, MO

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. We price all three so you can compare.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in St. Peters

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in St. Peters

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in St. Peters

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in St. Peters, MO

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Then the sewer smell shows up. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, which is why we look at the vent whenever the lateral checks out fine. Fixes range from a quick clearing to a new section of stack.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in St. Peters, MO

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Both save driveways and mature trees. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in St. Peters

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. Our phone is answered after hours. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. Sometimes the permanent fix waits for daylight. Until then, we tell you which fixtures are safe to use.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in St. Peters, MO

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Expansive clay soils are common in Missouri, and they swell and shrink with moisture, putting stress on foundations and buried lines. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.
