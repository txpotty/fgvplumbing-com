    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in St. Peters

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in St. Peters

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor.     Jammed Disposal Repair in St. Peters

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller.     Leak Detection and Repair in St. Peters, MO

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. Three of the four are fixable. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.
