    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in St. Peters, MO

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in St. Peters, MO

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Groundwater from limestone areas is especially hard, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. Scale builds up inside and the opening shrinks. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up.     PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in St. Peters

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.
