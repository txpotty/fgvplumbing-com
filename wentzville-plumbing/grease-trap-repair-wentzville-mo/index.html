    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Wentzville

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead.     Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Wentzville, MO

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. We put both options in writing with a price on each.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Wentzville

    A sewer smell near the trap means gas is getting out somewhere it should not. Most of the time it is a torn lid gasket or a dry floor drain nearby.     Flow Control Device Repair in Wentzville

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate.     Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Wentzville, MO

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. Once a riser cracks, rainwater and grit wash straight into the tank. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. A light-duty lid in a drive aisle will break eventually.

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Wentzville, MO

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. Busy fryers fill a trap fast.
