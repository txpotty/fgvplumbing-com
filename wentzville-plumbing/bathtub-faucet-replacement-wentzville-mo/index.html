    Replace or Keep Repairing in Wentzville

    Repeated repairs add up. A valve on its third cartridge in five years is probably worn inside, so one replacement beats more repairs. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    A tub that will not shut off needs water off now. Check behind the trim plate or an access panel for stops, and if there are none, shut the main and leave the drain open. We send the next open plumber. Broken stems are a common cause.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Wentzville

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Wentzville, MO

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.
