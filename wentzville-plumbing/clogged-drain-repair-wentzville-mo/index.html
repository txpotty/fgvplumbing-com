    Hydro jetting sends water at very high pressure through a nozzle that scrubs the pipe wall. It cleans, not just opens. We use it on lines with heavy grease, thick mineral scale or soft roots that a cable would only punch through and leave behind on the pipe walls. We check pipe condition first.

    Call once the plunger has failed. More effort rarely helps. Other signs it is time are more than one drain backing up, backups in odd places and clogs that keep coming back. Those usually point past the trap.

    Snaking uses a cable with a cutting or grabbing head. It breaks through the clog. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe wall, which removes the grease, scale and roots that a cable leaves behind and often keeps a line clear far longer. We use both, depending on the pipe.

    Most single fixture clogs take 30 to 90 minutes. A few run over. Lines with heavy buildup, poor access or a clog that has been treated with chemicals take more time because we have to work carefully. We tell you if it will run long.
