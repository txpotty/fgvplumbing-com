    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Wentzville

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in Wentzville

    Removing an old drop-in begins by slicing through the old caulk and backing off the clips from below.
