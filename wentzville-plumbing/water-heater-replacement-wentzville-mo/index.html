    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Wentzville

    Electric water heaters in Wentzville usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced.     Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Wentzville, MO

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Wentzville

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Wentzville

A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.
