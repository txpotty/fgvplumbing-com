    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Wentzville

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Wentzville

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Water heater replacements, sewer repairs and repiping usually need a permit where the city or county enforces a code. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Wentzville, MO

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. Upfront cost is higher. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Wentzville

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.
