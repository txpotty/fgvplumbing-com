    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Wentzville

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Wentzville, MO

    The basin is where the system lives, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. A larger basin means longer, less frequent cycles that spare the motor and switch, so we go bigger when the pit takes a laundry or several fixtures. Every lid gets sealed.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Wentzville

    Ejector pumps tend to run 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Signs of trouble include a pump that runs constantly, a grinding or humming noise, an alarm that keeps tripping and sewage odor near the pit, and any of those means it is time to call before the basement floor tells you. The float usually goes first.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Wentzville, MO

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer.
