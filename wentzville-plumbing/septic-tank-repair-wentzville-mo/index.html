    Leach Field Restoration in Wentzville, MO

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A box that has settled or cracked can push all the effluent into a single trench. Leveling or replacing the box often brings the field back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Wentzville, MO

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Wentzville

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. We measure sludge and scum on every maintenance visit, and pumping is due once solids fill roughly a third of the tank. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Wentzville

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter set up for a 4-inch building sewer. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and the fix after clearing depends on which one we pull out. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Septic Tee Repair in Wentzville

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Wentzville, MO

    Some tanks have no riser, so we look at the baffles through the small inspection port with a push camera. That saves digging up the whole lid. If the outlet tee is cracked or missing, we dig at that end only, then install a PVC tee with a filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.
