    Locating and Fixing Foundation Leaks in Wentzville, MO

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Wentzville

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. A cut cable can snap hard. Radar scanning maps the cables before any cut, and when the leak is too close to a tendon, we reroute the line instead.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Wentzville, MO

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. They aren't pressurized. A hydrostatic test shows whether the line holds water, and a camera run shows us the cracked fitting, offset joint or roots causing it. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we lay out the trade-offs.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Wentzville

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. Plastic pipe fails too. Most failures we find are at a fitting that was crimped short or a joint that pulled apart when the soil moved, not in the pipe wall itself. FGV Plumbing replaces the fitting with a proper crimp, expansion or solvent-weld connection so the line under your Wentzville foundation is sound again.
