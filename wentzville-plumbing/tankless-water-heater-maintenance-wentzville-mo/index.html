    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Wentzville, MO, Unit by Unit

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Wentzville

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. On these units we pull the air intake filter, wash it and check the flame rod for carbon buildup. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Wentzville

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Wentzville, MO

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Then we check the fan and the combustion air path. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Wentzville

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Wentzville

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. On a condensing unit the PVC or polypropylene vent should pitch back toward the heater. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Wentzville, MO

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. We check gas pressure with all units firing at once. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Wentzville

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. We replace spent media. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Wentzville, MO

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. Gas pressure at the inlet gets read with a manometer at full fire and checked against the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.
