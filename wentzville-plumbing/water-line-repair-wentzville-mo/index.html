    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Wentzville

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. With PEX, the fitting is the weak point most of the time. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, while PEX gets a fresh crimp ring or expansion fitting with the right tool for the brand.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Wentzville, MO

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rodents chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Wentzville

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, then swap a failed regulator for a new Watts or Zurn Wilkins valve when it reads too high. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Wentzville, MO

    Besides repairs, FGV Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, like a restaurant adding a dish line or a second restroom. Bigger meters need bigger pipe. We size it to the fixture load. Then we coordinate with the water provider on the tap and meter change, set the backflow assembly and get the inspection signed off, so the tenant is not held up waiting on paperwork.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Wentzville

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. A two-foot section is typical. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, then we look over the pipe on either side for more pitting before we backfill.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Wentzville

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. The new line travels between them without a trench. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, and most jobs wrap up in a single day.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Wentzville, MO

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Free-running water washes soil out from under walks and slabs fast. FGV Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. Most breaks are shut down quickly. If the repair will run long, a temporary bypass keeps water flowing to the house during the repair.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Wentzville

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Older brick homes in Missouri often still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains, which corrode and narrow over time. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. We price each one for you.
