    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Wentzville

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. High-efficiency condensing tanks reach 0.80 or more and can qualify for rebates.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Wentzville, MO

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Wentzville, MO

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. That is all part of the job. Downtime stays short.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Wentzville, MO

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Wentzville, MO

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Wentzville, MO

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.
