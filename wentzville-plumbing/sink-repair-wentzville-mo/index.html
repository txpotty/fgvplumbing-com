    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Wentzville, MO

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We run a camera or a longer cable into the wall arm and look for a sagging line or buildup where the sink ties into the tub drain. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Wentzville

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Wentzville

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Wentzville, MO

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Wentzville

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Wentzville

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. When the epoxy under the counter fails, the bowl drops a quarter inch, the caulk line splits, and water from the counter runs straight down into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Wentzville, MO

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. That points to the overflow gasket or the putty under the drain flange, since neither one sees water during a quick hand wash but both get soaked when you fill the sink. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Wentzville, MO

    Most sinks do not need replacing. Swapping a tailpiece or pop-up costs far less than a new sink, and you keep the bowl and faucet you already have. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.
