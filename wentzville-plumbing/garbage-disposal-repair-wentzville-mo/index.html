    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Wentzville

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor.     Noisy Disposal Repair in Wentzville

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Wentzville, MO

    InSinkErator makes the Badger line and the Evolution series, and we repair both.     Leak Detection and Repair in Wentzville, MO

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.
