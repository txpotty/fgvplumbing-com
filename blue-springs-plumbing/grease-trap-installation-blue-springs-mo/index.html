    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Blue Springs, MO

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Blue Springs

    On a new build-out, the grease trap gets decided before the slab is poured, not after. The drawings and the equipment list from your kitchen designer tell us what will drain where.     Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Blue Springs

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. The tech walks the kitchen and writes down every fixture that drains to the trap and how fast each one empties. From there we pick a hydromechanical unit or a gravity tank and figure out where it can sit with a straight shot to the building sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Blue Springs, MO

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. They fit indoors, under a sink or in a floor pit. The flow control is not optional. Without it, a dumped sink pushes water through faster than the unit is rated for and the grease goes out the outlet. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Grease Trap Brands We Install in Blue Springs

    Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura cover most of what we set in commercial kitchens, plus precast concrete tanks for the big outdoor interceptors. Each brand has its own lid gasket and baffle layout. Parts availability matters. We prefer models whose lids and gaskets are stocked nearby, because waiting a week on a part means waiting a week to reopen.

    Grease Trap Replacement and Upgrades in Blue Springs

    Plastic and steel traps both wear out, just in different ways. Steel rusts at the seams and the baffle welds. Plastic cracks. When we pull an old trap we cap the lines, haul it out, set the new unit on the same footprint where possible and reconnect with no-hub couplings so the next swap goes faster.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Blue Springs

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. They cost more up front. Plan for a dedicated circuit near the unit, which your electrician will need to run. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.

    Zurn Grease Trap Installation in Blue Springs

    Zurn's indoor grease interceptors cover the common sizes from small under-sink units up to 100 GPM, and we set enough of them to know where the gaskets give trouble. After a few years of hot water and scraping, the lid gasket is what usually leaks. We keep spares on the truck. We mount the supplied flow control on the inlet, at the height the instructions list, and vent it.
