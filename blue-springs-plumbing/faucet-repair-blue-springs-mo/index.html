    What We Fix on Faucets in Blue Springs, MO

    Disc Faucet Repair in Blue Springs

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Blue Springs, MO

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. Commercial cartridges are on the truck. Metering faucets get timed so they run long enough to rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Blue Springs, MO

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Look at the floor of the sink cabinet. We replace the O-rings and tighten the mounting hardware, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Blue Springs, MO

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long. Cartridges get matched for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Blue Springs, MO

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Blue Springs, MO

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. Each brand uses its own part number. We match it. Swapping a common cartridge like the Moen 1225 takes about half an hour, cleanup included.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Blue Springs, MO

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Blue Springs

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.
