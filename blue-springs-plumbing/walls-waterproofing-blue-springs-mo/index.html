    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Blue Springs, MO

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Blue Springs, MO

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Blue Springs, MO

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Rapid swings from mild to freezing weather expand and contract pipes and fittings, especially in older homes. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.
