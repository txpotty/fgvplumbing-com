    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Blue Springs

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. They cost more.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Blue Springs, MO

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Missouri homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Softener Loop Installation in Blue Springs, MO

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Blue Springs, MO

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. Outdoor hose bibs usually stay hard. No sense spending salt on the garden.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Blue Springs

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. We size for the house you have today.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Blue Springs, MO

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Blue Springs, MO

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. The valve's age decides it. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Blue Springs, MO

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. First step, turn the bypass handle. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.
