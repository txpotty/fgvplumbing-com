    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Blue Springs, MO

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. Exposed runs get heat cable. We install self-regulating cable that adjusts its output to the pipe temperature, plugged into a GFCI outlet, and cover it with insulation so it uses less power and keeps the pipe above freezing on the coldest nights.

    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Blue Springs

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and take the coldest air directly. Brass bodies crack. We repair or replace the damaged assembly, have it tested if your water utility requires it, and show you how to drain it or fit an insulated cover before the next freeze.

    Preventive Pipe Insulation in Blue Springs

    Foam pipe insulation is cheap and stops most freezes in attics and crawlspaces. Fit matters. Seams get taped and corners get mitered, because one bare fitting is often where the freeze starts.

    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Blue Springs, MO

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Blue Springs
