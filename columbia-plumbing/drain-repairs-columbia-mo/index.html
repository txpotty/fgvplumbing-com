    Main Line Drain Repair in Columbia

    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. The lowest fixture backs up first. Roots, cracks from ground movement and collapsed sections are the common causes, and we confirm which one on camera, then repair it with a spot dig, a trenchless liner or a section replacement depending on what the footage shows. We show you before we quote.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Columbia

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and most of them are obvious once the camera or a pulled trap shows the inside. We repair the part that catches debris.

    Storm Drain Repair in Columbia, MO

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Sudden downpours often overwhelm older sewer systems, which can push water back up through basement floor drains. Leaves and silt clog them. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and repair broken pipe so the runoff reaches daylight instead of soaking the soil next to your foundation. We check the discharge end every time.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Columbia, MO

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, so we cut out the failing section and replace it with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.

    Kitchen Branch Line Repair in Columbia

    This branch takes more grease and food than any other line in most homes. Buildup gets deep. We replace cracked tailpieces, fix slip nuts and correct trap height, and when grease has built up deep in the branch, we jet it clean and check the slope so it does not come right back. Dishwasher and disposal connections get checked.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Columbia

    Clearing a clog usually runs $150 to $400. Pipe repairs cost more. Main line repairs can be anywhere from $800 to $5,000 or more, and excavation pushes the price toward the top of that range. We price it once we know the cause.

    Repeat clogs come from something in the pipe catching debris. Grease or scale is common. Poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a cracked fitting with a rough edge will snag paper and hair until the line closes off again a few weeks after each cleaning. We find it on video.

    We install PVC and ABS for most new drain work. Some jobs call for cast iron. Cast iron still makes sense in some multi-story walls where noise matters, and we tie new pipe into existing metal drains with listed transition fittings. All materials are code-listed.

    Slow drains, gurgling and sewer smell are the big three warnings. Drips in the cabinet count too. A drain that clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared, or a stain on the ceiling below a tub or shower, means the pipe or a fitting needs repair rather than another cleaning. Catch it early.

    Tightening a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is fair game. Hand snakes are fine too. Anything inside a wall, under a floor or involving cast iron, or a leak that keeps returning after you have tightened it, is a job for a licensed plumber with the right tools. Skip the caustic cleaners.

    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Bigger changes might. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Columbia may require a plumbing permit, depending on local rules and how much of the system is being changed. We pull it when one applies.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. The whole inside gets cleaned. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, but a jet nozzle strips buildup off the entire pipe wall. We camera first on old or brittle pipe.

    Clearing a simple clog takes an hour or two. Repairs run longer. Replacing a section of main line or opening a wall to swap a cast iron stack may run a day or longer with access and inspections setting the pace. We give you a timeline with the price.
