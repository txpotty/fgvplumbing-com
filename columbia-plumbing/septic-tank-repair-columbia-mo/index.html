    Septic Tee Repair in Columbia

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Columbia, MO

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age. Basements are common in many areas, so sump pumps and drain tile are an important part of keeping homes dry. Roots and settling ground split joints and crush the pipe. We replace the bad run with 4-inch PVC, bedded in sand and sloped a quarter inch per foot. Short sections take an afternoon.

    After-Hours Septic Backup Calls in Columbia

    Late-night backups usually start in the lowest drain in the house, often a basement floor drain or a first-floor shower. Our on-call tech carries a jetter and spare floats, which means most calls end with the drains working. Bad nights end with a pump truck. Either way, you know what failed before we leave.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Columbia, MO

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Leach Field Restoration in Columbia, MO

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A tilted or crushed D-box can send everything to one trench and drown it. Leveling or replacing the box often brings the field back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Columbia

    Sewage in the shower pan on a Sunday is an emergency, and we treat it like one.     Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. Our techs rebuild linear aerators and swap rotary ones, and we clear diffusers that have plugged with sludge. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.
