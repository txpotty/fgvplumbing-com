    What We Install in Columbia, MO

    Water Softener Installation in Columbia, MO

    Groundwater from limestone areas is especially hard, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. A hardness test at the kitchen tap, in grains per gallon, tells us whether a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner fits your usage. The bypass valve matters. It lets you shut the unit off for service without losing water to the house.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Columbia, MO

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. In a repipe, fewer fittings means fewer hidden joints and fewer chances for a leak to soak a wall. It can also stretch when water freezes. Hard freezes happen every winter in Missouri, and the northern part of the state usually sees longer cold spells than the south. Stretch or not, we still insulate runs in exterior walls and crawl spaces.

    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Columbia, MO

    Water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces and pool heaters all pull from the same gas service. Each branch is sized from the total BTU load and the run length so the last appliance on the line is not starved. Every new line is pressure tested. Bigger loads sometimes need a meter upgrade, and we set that up with the gas utility.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Columbia, MO

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Columbia, MO

    Electric tankless heaters pull serious amperage, often 120 amps or more for a whole-house unit. Before we quote one, we check that your panel and service can carry that load, and we bring in a licensed electrician when they cannot. Plumbing is the easy part. Flow and temperature get dialed in so a running dishwasher does not turn the shower cold.

    Backflow Preventer Installation in Columbia

    Irrigation systems and fire sprinkler lines are the usual reasons a home needs a backflow preventer. It keeps fertilizer-laced lawn water from being pulled back into the drinking supply when a main break or hydrant flush drops pressure. Type matters. We match the assembly to the hazard and the local rule, then test it before we leave.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Columbia

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. We size before we sell. The pump gets matched to the fill rate, wired to a dedicated circuit and piped out through a check valve.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Columbia

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. We run either a manifold with home runs to each fixture, which balances pressure, or a trunk-and-branch layout when the walls make home runs impractical. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.
