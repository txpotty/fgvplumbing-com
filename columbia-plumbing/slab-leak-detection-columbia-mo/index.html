    Why Locating First Saves Money in Columbia

    Detection usually costs a few hundred dollars. A wrong guess costs more. Breaking up a floor in the wrong place means paying for concrete work, flooring and a second opening, while a precise mark keeps the job to one opening or a reroute that leaves the slab alone in your Columbia property.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Columbia

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Non-Invasive Slab Leak Locating in Columbia, MO

    The floor gets opened last. Pressure tests, line tracing, thermal scans and acoustic listening all happen from the surface in your Columbia, MO home, without drilling or cutting anything. Only after two methods agree do we mark the spot and talk about repair options, including ones that skip the concrete entirely.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Columbia, MO

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Slab Leak Price Ranges in Columbia, MO

    Locating a slab leak usually runs $150 to $450.     Pressure Testing and Isolation in Columbia

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Columbia, MO home.
