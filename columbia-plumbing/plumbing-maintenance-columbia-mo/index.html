    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Columbia

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Sump Pump Testing in Columbia, MO

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. A working sump pump and a backwater valve are smart protection for any home with a finished basement. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Backup pumps get tested too.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Columbia

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Hard water drops minerals that settle on the bottom of the tank, where they trap heat, waste fuel and make that crackling sound every time the burner or elements fire. We flush it out. We descale tankless units through their service valves. Records support warranty claims.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Columbia, MO

    A working sump pump and a backwater valve are smart protection for any home with a finished basement. A sump pump sits idle for months and then has to work hard all at once, so we test the float switch, clean debris out of the pit and confirm the discharge line runs clear to daylight. The check valve gets a look. We test any backup pump.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Columbia, MO

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. We check visible gas piping and appliance connectors for corrosion and leaks, confirm the shutoff valves turn, and note any old flexible connectors that should be replaced before they crack. We note pipe types. Older pipe types get flagged with repipe choices.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Columbia

    Mid-century homes typically have copper supply lines, while newer houses rely on PEX and plastic drain pipe. Buried and under-slab water lines can wear from soil movement and hard water until they start leaking where nobody can see it, sometimes for months. Warm floors are a clue. So is a meter that spins with everything off. We use acoustic and thermal tools to find the spot.

    Water Softener Service in Columbia

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.
