    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Choices in Columbia, MO

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Columbia, MO

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Columbia, MO

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Columbia, MO

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Columbia, MO

    Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated garages are the usual trouble spots when temperatures drop sharply. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, so we fix it and protect it from the next freeze. Then we test it.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Columbia, MO

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, and we check the water heater connections and shutoffs since they often corrode too. Look at the joints.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Columbia

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Groundwater from limestone areas is especially mineral-heavy, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Columbia, MO

    Pinhole leaks start small. First a damp spot, then drywall damage. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, and when one shows up, more are often on the way along the same lines. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Columbia

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.
