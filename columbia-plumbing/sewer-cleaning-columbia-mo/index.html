    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Columbia, MO

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. A greasy main slowly loses diameter until it cannot carry a full washer load or a bathtub draining at once, and jetting is the right fix.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Columbia, MO

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Mid-century homes typically have copper supply lines, while newer houses use PEX and plastic drain pipe. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Columbia, MO

    History predicts the next backup. We set a cleaning interval. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.
