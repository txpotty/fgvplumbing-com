    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Columbia

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Columbia, MO

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FGV Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Columbia, MO

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Carbon tanks cost extra. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Columbia

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Columbia, MO

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. Disposal is included. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Columbia

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. We test the water, ask about daily use and choose a capacity that lets the system regenerate roughly once a week. That keeps salt use reasonable. Every detail is handled on one visit.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Columbia, MO

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. We install bigger tanks or twin alternating units for businesses, which keeps soft water flowing during regeneration. Dishwashers and boilers last longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Columbia

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.
