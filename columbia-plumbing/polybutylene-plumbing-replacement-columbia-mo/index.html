    PEX Pipe Installation in Columbia, MO

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Columbia

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter.     Copper Pipe Replacement in Columbia

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. It costs more. We review your water and any history of copper pinholes nearby before recommending it over PEX.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Columbia

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Columbia

    Most PEX replacements of polybutylene cost $5,000 to $12,000 for an average home. Copper costs more, and yard service lines often add $1,500 to $4,000.

    Most policies do not pay for replacing old pipe, though they may cover sudden water damage. Some insurers raise rates or decline coverage for homes with polybutylene.
