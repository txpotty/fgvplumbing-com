    Guest suites, detached studios and additions often sit far from the main water heater. Long hot lines waste water and heat, so a nearby electric tankless is often the better choice. Sizing matches the fixtures. No vent needed.

    Whole-Home Electric Tankless Installation in Columbia

    Whole-home electric units like the Stiebel Eltron Tempra and Rheem RTEX can feed several fixtures at once when sized right.     Point-of-Use Electric Tankless Installation in Columbia

    A guest bath or a far kitchen sink can take a minute to get hot water from the main heater. Installing a small unit at the fixture gives hot water in seconds and wastes less water. They fit under the sink. Small circuits are usually enough.

    Whole-Home Electric Tankless Systems in Columbia, MO

    Whole-home coverage in Columbia, MO means one unit big enough for your busiest hour.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Columbia

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and appliances. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. It is a short service.

    Converting from Tank to Electric Tankless in Columbia, MO

    Converting a tank to electric tankless frees floor space and ends standby loss. The old tank comes out, the new unit goes on the wall and the lines get rerouted, with new circuits run by an electrician. Plan on one day. The setup ends up cleaner.

    Cost Comparison: Electric Tankless vs. Traditional in Columbia, MO

    Upfront, electric tankless usually costs more than a tank because of the electrical work. A tankless can outlive two tanks and skips standby loss, which narrows the gap over time. Usage decides it. You get a real comparison first.
