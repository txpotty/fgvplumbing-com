    PEX Pipe Replacement in Columbia, MO

We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Columbia

    Most PEX repipes for Missouri homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper repipes usually run 20 to 40 percent more.

    You get a written workmanship warranty covering labor and materials. Details are spelled out up front. Our repipes use Uponor PEX-A or domestic Type L copper, and certified PEX-A jobs also get the manufacturer's long-term warranty.

    Groundwater from limestone areas is especially mineral-heavy, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. Hard water deposits calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe over time. Pressure falls as scale builds. Scale also speeds corrosion in metal lines, which is why PEX-A plus a softener or filter added during a repipe can protect the new system and your fixtures.

    Rusty or brown water, low pressure at several fixtures and repeated pinhole leaks are the classic signs. Noisy pipes can be another. Homes with galvanized, polybutylene or Kitec should plan a repipe before a failure does it for them, because those pipes fail system-wide.
