    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Columbia, MO

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Locating comes before cutting. The main tools are a ground microphone and thermal imager. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Columbia home.
