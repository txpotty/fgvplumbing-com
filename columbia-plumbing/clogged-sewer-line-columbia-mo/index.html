    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Columbia

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. Cabling alone is not enough. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. The results hold up far better.

    Grease and Sludge Buildup Removal in Columbia, MO

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Layer after layer builds up.     Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Columbia

    Clay and older cast iron sewer pipe have a joint every few feet. Each joint is an opening. Roots from trees and shrubs push into the gaps and fill the pipe, then we cut them out and camera the line for damage. Ongoing root problems may call for a treatment schedule or lining.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Columbia

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. It shows us everything.
