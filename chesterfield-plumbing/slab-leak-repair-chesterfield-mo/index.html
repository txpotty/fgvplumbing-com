    Larger jobs often need one. Small fixes usually don't. Rerouting, main line changes and repairs that involve structural work typically require a plumbing permit from local authorities, and we file the application so the repair is compliant. FGV Plumbing meets the inspector too.

    A slab leak is in pipe running within or under the concrete foundation. You can't see it. Leaks in walls, ceilings or exposed pipe usually leave a stain or a drip you can follow, while a slab leak hides under the floor and takes locating gear to find.
