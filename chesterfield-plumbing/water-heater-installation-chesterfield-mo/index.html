    Expansion Tank Installation in Chesterfield, MO

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Chesterfield

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Chesterfield

    Homes with a pressure reducing valve or check valve usually need one. It keeps pressure spikes off your plumbing.

    Start with correct sizing and a thermostat set at 120 degrees. Insulating the first few feet of hot pipe and flushing the tank once a year both help, and a tankless model cuts standby loss.

    The job involves gas, venting, electrical and pressure safety. A licensed plumber installs it safely and protects your warranty.

    Popping or rumbling, rust-colored water, leaks or an age past ten years are the common clues. One of those alone is reason enough to have it checked.
