    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Chesterfield

    Pipe Bursting in Chesterfield, MO

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Sectional Point Repair in Chesterfield

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in Chesterfield laterals.
