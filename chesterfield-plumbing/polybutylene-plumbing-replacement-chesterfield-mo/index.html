    Why Polybutylene Fails in Chesterfield

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and fixtures. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
