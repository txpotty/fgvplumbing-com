    Most home inspections skip the sewer line. That is a risk. We clean the line first so the camera can see the pipe walls clearly, then record the full length and provide a written report that buyers can use to negotiate repairs or credits.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Chesterfield, MO

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. Cables only open a path. High-pressure water strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes them toward the street, and with the right settings it is safe for PVC and older clay alike.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Chesterfield, MO

    Roots are the top cause of blockages in established neighborhoods. Waste catches on them. After cutting the mass with a root saw, we jet the line to clear root hairs and leave the pipe clean, then show each entry point on camera.

    Clearing Household Laterals in Chesterfield

    Most residential sewer clogs have three or four usual causes. Roots lead the list. Wipes, grease and roots cause most household clogs, and a camera tells us which method will work best, so the fix lasts.

    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Chesterfield, MO

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Listen for gurgling. Sewer odors, slow drains in several rooms and water rising in the tub when the toilet flushes are all warning signs.

    Restaurant and Shop Sewer Lines in Chesterfield, MO

    Sewer Line Odor Remediation in Chesterfield, MO

    Sewer smells mean something is wrong. Waste may be trapped. Persistent odors from drains or the yard often come from trapped waste, a blockage or a damaged line letting gas escape, and our cleaning removes the source while the camera finds any break.
