    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Chesterfield, MO

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. Fine soil plugs it over time. Expansive clay soils are common in Missouri, and they swell and shrink with moisture, putting stress on foundations and buried lines. We confirm it drains to daylight or a sump.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Chesterfield

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and each one shows up clearly on a camera or when we pull the trap. The fix goes where the clog starts.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Chesterfield

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Some come back fast.     Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Chesterfield, MO

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too.     Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Chesterfield, MO

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Every repair is done to code.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Chesterfield, MO

If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.
