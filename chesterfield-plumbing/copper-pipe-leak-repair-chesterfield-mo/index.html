    A simple solder repair usually runs $200 to $500. Access adds cost. A slab leak that needs specialized detection and repair could range from $800 to $3,000 or more, with reroutes at the top of that range. We price it once we know the cause.

    Clamps and epoxy putty can stop a drip for a day. Temporary fixes fail. A proper repair means cutting out the thin copper and soldering or pressing in new pipe, and a DIY patch that lets go while you are at work can flood a room in the time it takes to drive home. Treat it as a stopgap.

    We work with Type K, L and M copper from all standard manufacturers. Each meets ASTM specs. We repair whatever is already in your walls, and we tie new copper or PEX into it with fittings rated for the pipe size and the transition. We default to Type L.
