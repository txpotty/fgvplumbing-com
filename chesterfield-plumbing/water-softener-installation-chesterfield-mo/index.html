    Long-Lasting Water Softener Installs in Chesterfield, MO

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Chesterfield

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Chesterfield

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Chesterfield

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Chesterfield, MO

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. We remove the old unit, check the loop and drain for problems and install a newer model that fits your current hardness and household size. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Chesterfield, MO

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Chesterfield

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. Connections and setup included. Existing loops make it faster.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Chesterfield

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Chesterfield, MO

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. Taste improves fast. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.
