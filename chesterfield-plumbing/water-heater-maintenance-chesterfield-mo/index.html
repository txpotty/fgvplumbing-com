    Tankless Descaling in Chesterfield, MO

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and fixtures. We close the isolation valves, connect a small pump and circulate descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer recommends, then flush it with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too. We also check the burner and venting.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Chesterfield

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. Both elements get tested. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Electric Element Service in Chesterfield, MO

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. Good parts stay in.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in Chesterfield

    Wrong temperature means a part is off. On electric units we test the thermostats and elements for proper resistance and accurate shutoff, and on gas units we inspect the burner, the pilot or igniter and the gas control valve. Rapid swings from mild to freezing weather stress pipes and fittings, especially in older homes. Faulty parts waste energy. You run out sooner.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Chesterfield, MO

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. Leak checks, flame inspection, burner cleaning and a draft test are part of every gas visit. Carbon monoxide is the concern. We also test the thermocouple.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Chesterfield, MO

    Anode Rod Checks in Chesterfield
