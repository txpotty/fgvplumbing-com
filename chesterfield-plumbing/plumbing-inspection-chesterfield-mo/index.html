    Plumbing Inspection Work in Chesterfield, MO

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Chesterfield, MO

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Chesterfield

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Chesterfield, MO

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. Plan on an hour or so. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Chesterfield, MO

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Chesterfield, MO

    Older homes in Chesterfield often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. Our camera shows each defect, and the onboard counter tells us exactly how far from the cleanout it sits. Then we locate it. With depth and position marked, repair pricing gets accurate, and choosing between a spot fix, a liner or replacement gets easier.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Chesterfield, MO

    Rapid swings from mild to freezing weather stress pipes and fittings, especially in older homes. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. Insulation gets checked too. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Chesterfield, MO

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. Backflow devices, grease traps, large heaters, flushometers and the main sewer are all part of it. Inspectors like documentation. Managers use the report to fix problems before an inspector or water provider flags them.

    Water Heater Inspections in Chesterfield

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    We check water supply lines, drain lines, fixtures, the water heater, sump pump, garbage disposal and exposed gas lines. We test how drains flow. Our plumber also checks for leaks, corrosion and vent issues, measures pressure and writes up the condition of every component.
