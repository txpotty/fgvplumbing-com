    Deep Well Pump Repair in Chesterfield

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out.     Constant Pressure System Repair in Chesterfield, MO

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. When they fault, the drive shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Chesterfield, MO

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Sudden downpours often overload older sewer systems, which can push water back up through basement floor drains. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. Arrestors are cheap.
