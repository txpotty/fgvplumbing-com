    Gas Leak Detection in Chesterfield

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. Our plumbers isolate the system section by section, sweep it with an electronic detector and confirm the exact spot with soap solution. After we locate the leak, we price the repair in writing.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Chesterfield

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Chesterfield

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. An electronic detector does the rest. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Chesterfield, MO

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Uncoated brass connectors from decades ago crack at the flare fittings and should be replaced on sight. New coated connectors go in. We also add a reachable shutoff and a drip leg where needed, and confirm the new connector is rated for that appliance's BTU input.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Chesterfield, MO

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    After-Hours Gas Line Repair in Chesterfield

    Gas problems do not wait for business hours, and losing heat or hot water overnight is a real problem. We answer late calls. Once everyone is out and the gas company has secured things, we send a plumber to find the leak, fix it and test the system. We coordinate with the utility on the meter.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Chesterfield

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Chesterfield, MO

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Line Repair in Chesterfield

    Small fitting or connector repairs typically cost $150 to $450. Section replacements run $400 to $1,200, and buried line replacement can reach $1,000 to $4,000 or more.

    Most gas piping changes and all new gas lines in Chesterfield, MO need a permit. Simple connector swaps often do not. The permit means an inspector checks the sizing, materials and pressure test, and we handle the filing and meet the inspector so you do not have to.

    Leaks at the meter or on the utility's service line belong to the gas company. Do not try to fix it yourself. Leave the property and call the utility, and after they address their side we can fix anything on your side of the meter.

    Our plumbers work with black iron, coated steel, copper where local code allows it, and CSST in homes across Missouri. Each needs the right fittings. The right thread compound, matching CSST fittings and proper pipe supports are what keep a repair tight for years.

    Utilities lock the meter when they find a leak or a failed test. Service stays off until a licensed plumber fixes it and the system passes a test.

    Every gas line repair we make includes a written warranty on parts and labor. Each one is pressure tested before we leave.

    There is no required yearly inspection for most homes. A look every few years helps older houses. It is worth a check if your home has old uncoated brass connectors, buried steel yard lines from decades ago or appliances that are burning with yellow or lazy flames, since those are common warning signs.

    Gas lines do not need routine service the way a water heater does. Checks still help. On other service calls we can check exposed gas pipe, connectors and valves and flag old connectors, missing drip legs or rust before it becomes a leak.
