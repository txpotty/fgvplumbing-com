    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Chesterfield, MO

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. That is the moment to check it. A cracked or sunken flange gets repaired before the new bowl goes down, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Chesterfield

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. We look closely before quoting.     Chipped or Stained Bowl Replacement in Chesterfield, MO

    Deep chips in the glaze collect bacteria, and hard water rings that have etched the surface will not come out with any cleaner. Replacing the bowl solves both problems.     Cracked Bolt Hole Repair in Chesterfield

    Hairline cracks near the bolt holes often start when someone tightens the closet bolts too far, and they spread a little with every flush and every sit. Sealants do not hold for long.
