    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in Chesterfield, MO

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Chesterfield

    Electric water heaters in Chesterfield usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Chesterfield, MO

    A standard tank replacement costs the least up front in Chesterfield, MO. Tankless and hybrid units cost more up front but can lower operating costs, and tankless often lasts 15 to 20 years versus about 8 to 12 for a tank. We price all three.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Chesterfield

    Businesses in Chesterfield run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. We schedule around your hours.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Chesterfield

    Mid-century homes typically have copper supply lines, while newer houses use PEX and plastic drain pipe. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. Two-person crew, always.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Chesterfield, MO

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. Repair stays an option.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Chesterfield

    We use Bradford White often on our jobs because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When we replace one, we match the fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty might still cover the tank, and register the new unit so the warranty is on file from day one. We file the warranty.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Chesterfield, MO

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Chesterfield

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.

That might mean a model with a better anode, a drain pan with a line to the outside or an expansion tank on a closed system, and every one of those choices gets explained on the quote before we start. You see it all up front.
