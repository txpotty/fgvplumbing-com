    Touchless Faucet Installation in Lee's Summit

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. The control box mounts under the sink, up off the cabinet floor where a small leak will not soak it, and it runs on batteries or a plug-in transformer. Scale is hard on solenoids, and we install the inline filter when the maker calls for one. Sensor range gets tested last.

    Restaurant and Office Faucets in Lee's Summit, MO

    Restaurant pre-rinse units mount to the wall and the sink deck. The spring arm and spray valve take a beating, so we anchor the wall bracket into blocking and use check valves on the supply lines. Hand sinks get wrist blades or sensors. Night installs keep the kitchen open.

    Garage and Workshop Faucets in Lee's Summit

    Workshop and garage sinks get abused. These need metal handles and a solid anchor, since a yanked hose will twist a loose faucet off its supply lines. We check the flow at full open. If the tub drains slow, we clear the trap while we are there.

    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Installation in Lee's Summit

    Hose bibs get more abuse than any indoor faucet. Hard freezes happen every winter in Missouri, and the northern part of the state usually sees longer cold spells than the south. Where pipes can freeze, we install a frost-free sillcock that closes the valve inside the warm part of the wall. Code calls for a vacuum breaker on every hose bib. We use models with the breaker built into the body.

    Laundry Sink Faucet Installation in Lee's Summit

    Laundry sinks get a faucet with a threaded spout. Deck or wall models go in with a built-in vacuum breaker, because a hose sitting in dirty wash water can siphon back into the supply. We clip the supply lines so nothing rubs.

    Brand-Specific Faucet Installation in Lee's Summit

We follow the instruction sheet for the exact model number, because an install that skips a step or reuses an old part can void the warranty. Grohe and Brizo come across our trucks most weeks too.
