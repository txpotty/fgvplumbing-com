    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Lee's Summit, MO

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. Venting changes too. Both are on the estimate.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Lee's Summit

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. We weigh both. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lee's Summit, MO

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Lee's Summit, MO homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. Panel capacity gets checked up front.
