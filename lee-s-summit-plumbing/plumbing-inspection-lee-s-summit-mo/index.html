    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Lee's Summit, MO

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Lee's Summit

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Lee's Summit, MO

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter.     Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Lee's Summit

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.
