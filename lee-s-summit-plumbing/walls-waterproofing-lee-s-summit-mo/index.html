    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Lee's Summit

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything.     Waterproofing for Older Homes in Lee's Summit, MO

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago.     Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Lee's Summit, MO

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.
