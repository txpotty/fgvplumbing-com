    Replacing an Old Disposal in Lee's Summit, MO

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Old gaskets go stiff. We haul the old unit away.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Lee's Summit

    Most households in Lee's Summit do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Wiring and testing come last. We listen for a smooth spin and watch the drain clear.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Lee's Summit

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Lee's Summit, MO

    If your old disposal quits with a sink full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. Our trucks carry common 1/2 and 3/4 HP models, mounting hardware and cord kits, so we do not need a second trip to the supply house before the sink drains again. Most swaps take about an hour.

    Disposal Upgrades in Lee's Summit, MO

    Upgrading from an old 1/3 HP unit to something quieter is a common request. We disconnect and remove the old disposal, check the trap and drain arm for corrosion, and install the new unit with a new mount, so the upgrade does not inherit the old unit's worn parts. Old drain arms often need replacing. Rust works from inside galvanized pipe.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Lee's Summit, MO

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We confirm the circuit can carry the motor and the drain can handle the flow, because a strong unit pushing into a flat trap arm will still clog. Bigger units are heavier. We support them while mounting.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Lee's Summit, MO

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    InSinkErator Model Selection in Lee's Summit, MO

    We help you pick between the Badger and Evolution lines based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have. Badger units are compact and simple, while Evolution units are taller and heavier, with more grind stages and better sound insulation. Cabinet size often decides it. We measure before you buy.
