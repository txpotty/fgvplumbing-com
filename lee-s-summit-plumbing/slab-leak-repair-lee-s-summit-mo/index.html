    Locating and Repairing Slab Leaks in Lee's Summit, MO

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Lee's Summit, MO

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. Tunneling solves that. A pit next to the foundation lets the crew tunnel under the slab by hand to the pipe, with shoring as needed, so your interior floors and cabinets stay untouched. Many Lee's Summit, MO homes are good candidates.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Lee's Summit, MO

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Lee's Summit

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. A reroute bypasses it. The bad run stays in the ground unused while a new line goes overhead or in the walls, where it can be reached later. Lee's Summit homes with original plumbing see this often.

    Slab Leak Detection in Lee's Summit

    The first test is the water meter. Every fixture gets shut off. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Emergency Slab Leak Services in Lee's Summit

    A burst line under the slab can push water through grout lines in a few hours. Close your main valve. A crew comes out to isolate the broken line, cap it so the rest of the house has water, and make a temporary or permanent repair depending on what we find. That goes for anywhere in Lee's Summit.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Lee's Summit

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold lines show no heat. For those, an acoustic sensor listens through the concrete for the sound of water escaping, and we mark the spot where it peaks, whether the house is an older ranch or a newer two-story. Both tools ride on every Lee's Summit truck.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Lee's Summit, MO

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is finished level with the floor.
