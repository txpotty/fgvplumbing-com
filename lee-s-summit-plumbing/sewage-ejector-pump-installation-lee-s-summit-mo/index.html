    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Lee's Summit, MO

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lee's Summit, MO

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lee's Summit

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Emergency Ejector Pump Replacement in Lee's Summit, MO

    A dead ejector pump takes every below-grade fixture out of service until it is fixed. Stop using those fixtures, including the washer if it drains to the pit, then call (816) 330-2338 and we aim to have a plumber and pump at your door the same day. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Lee's Summit

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. Sudden downpours often overwhelm older sewer systems, which can push water back up through basement floor drains. Backup power can help.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Lee's Summit, MO

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars send gray water down that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.
