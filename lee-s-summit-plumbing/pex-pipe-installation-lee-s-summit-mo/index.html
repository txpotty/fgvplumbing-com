    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Lee's Summit, MO

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorinated water breaks the pipe and its plastic fittings down from within, which means it can look perfectly fine until a fitting suddenly fails. Many insurers ask whether you have it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Lee's Summit

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Lee's Summit, MO

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. We carry the common sizes. Most emergency PEX repairs in Lee's Summit, MO come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Lee's Summit, MO

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Lee's Summit, MO

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.
