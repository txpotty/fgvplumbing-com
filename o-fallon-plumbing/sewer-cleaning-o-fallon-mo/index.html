    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in O'Fallon, MO

    Some lines need a look first. Fragile pipe gets the camera before the cutter. A camera run before cleaning shows whether the line has roots, a broken section or a grease plug, which tells us whether to cable, jet or stop and talk about repair.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in O'Fallon

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in O'Fallon, MO

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Apartment and Strip Center Sewers in O'Fallon, MO

    Shared laterals carry heavy loads. Several units, one pipe. We set up jetting on a schedule that fits your pump-out cycle and do the work when the building is quiet, with a written report and a video file on request.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in O'Fallon, MO

    History predicts the next backup. Repeat lines get a schedule. A line that has backed up before can be cleaned on a set schedule, often every year or two, so it never stops again without warning.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in O'Fallon, MO

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in O'Fallon, MO
