    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in O'Fallon, MO

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground.     Water Pressure Restoration in O'Fallon

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first.     Emergency Water Line Repair in O'Fallon, MO

    PEX Water Line Repair in O'Fallon, MO

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rodents chew it.
