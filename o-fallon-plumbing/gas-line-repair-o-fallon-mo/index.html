    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in O'Fallon, MO

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. Once the coating cracks, the pipe starts to corrode, and wet soil from sprinklers or a low spot in the yard speeds that up a great deal. We call 811 before digging. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in O'Fallon

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in O'Fallon

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. An electronic detector does the rest. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Propane Gas Line Service in O'Fallon, MO

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in O'Fallon

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.
