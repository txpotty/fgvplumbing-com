    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in O'Fallon, MO

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FGV Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in O'Fallon, MO

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Carbon tanks cost extra. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in O'Fallon, MO

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in O'Fallon

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in O'Fallon

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. We handle all of it. Existing loops make it faster.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in O'Fallon

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in O'Fallon

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron changes the math. You get a walkthrough of the settings.
