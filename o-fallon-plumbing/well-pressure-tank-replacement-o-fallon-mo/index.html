    Solid Pressure Tanks for O'Fallon, MO Homes

    FGV Plumbing replaces well pressure tanks, the steel tank that holds water under air pressure between the pump and your faucets. Inside is a rubber bladder or diaphragm that separates air from water. Once that bladder tears, the tank fills with water, the air cushion disappears, and the pump starts and stops every few seconds. Pumps hate that.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in O'Fallon

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. First step, kill the breaker to the pump.     Emergency Well Pressure Tank Replacement in O'Fallon

    A tank that bursts or a pump that runs nonstop needs attention fast. Turn the pump breaker off right away. A pump that keeps running against a failed system can overheat and burn out, and we aim to swap the tank before the pump suffers. Water back tonight.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in O'Fallon

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. A leak from the tank itself cannot. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, so a torn bladder or rusted seam means a new tank, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in O'Fallon, MO

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. It is one of the most common tanks we install. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, which helps the tank resist rust from the inside, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in O'Fallon, MO

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Multi-tank setups are common. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, while planning the work so the building keeps water. Larger tanks need two people.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in O'Fallon

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. The connection height and size usually match. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in O'Fallon, MO

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. Common sizes are easy for us to get. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, then confirming the gauge, switch and drawdown line up with the pump you have. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in O'Fallon

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. A new tank can reveal tired fittings. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, and give you options for doing the tank alone or the tank plus its fittings. Your call.
