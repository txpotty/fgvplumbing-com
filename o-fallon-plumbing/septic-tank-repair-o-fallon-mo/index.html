    Septic Tank Leak Repair in O'Fallon

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. We pump it down and look at the walls and seams with a light, and a dye test in the drains shows where water comes in or goes out. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    Emergency Septic Repair in O'Fallon

    Sewage in the shower pan on a Sunday is an emergency, and we treat it like one. The first thing we ask is whether the tank has a riser we can reach, because uncovering a buried lid can add an hour or more. Stop using water until the tech arrives. Then we find the blockage, clear it and tell you what caused it.

    Septic Tee Repair in O'Fallon

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in O'Fallon, MO

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in O'Fallon

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub tend to mean something is stuck in the line before the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter set up for a 4-inch building sewer. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and what we do next depends on what comes out of the pipe. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Leach Field Restoration in O'Fallon, MO

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A box that has settled or cracked can push all the effluent into a single trench. A new or leveled box brings a lot of fields back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in O'Fallon, MO

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. We rebuild or replace linear and rotary aerators, plus clean out diffusers clogged with solids. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in O'Fallon, MO

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.
