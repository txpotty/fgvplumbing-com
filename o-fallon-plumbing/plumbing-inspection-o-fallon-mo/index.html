    Preventive Maintenance Checks in O'Fallon, MO

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. Plan on an hour or so. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in O'Fallon

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. We document each unit. Water heaters, fixtures and sewer lines get recorded unit by unit, and commercial inspections can add backflow assemblies and grease trap lines, which helps owners budget for maintenance and larger repairs.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in O'Fallon, MO

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in O'Fallon

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in O'Fallon, MO

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Some need replacing soon. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in O'Fallon

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.
