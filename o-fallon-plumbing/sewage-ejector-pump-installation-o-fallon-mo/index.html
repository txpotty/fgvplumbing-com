    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in O'Fallon

    Residential ejector systems in O'Fallon serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer.     Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in O'Fallon, MO

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Redundancy matters here.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in O'Fallon

    Zoeller's sewage line runs from light residential to heavy duty, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches.     Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in O'Fallon

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.
