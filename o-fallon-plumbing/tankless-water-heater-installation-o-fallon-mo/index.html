    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in O'Fallon

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. We weigh both. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in O'Fallon, MO

    Businesses in O'Fallon, MO with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank.     Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in O'Fallon

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. That narrows the model list fast.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in O'Fallon, MO

    Upfront, a tankless install in O'Fallon, MO costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows. Less standby loss, a life of up to 20 years versus roughly 10 for a tank, and replaceable parts all count over the years. We price both side by side.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in O'Fallon

    Conversions in O'Fallon tend to run a full workday. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, the gas line is upsized and tested, a new vent goes out through a wall or roof, and the unit is mounted, plumbed with isolation valves and fired. Hot water is back by evening. Inspection follows within days.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in O'Fallon

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in O'Fallon, MO

    Replacing an old tankless unit tends to go faster than a conversion. Since the gas line and vent are already there, most swaps mean matching connections, inspecting the old vent and mounting the new heater. Vent sizes can change by brand. We check before ordering.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in O'Fallon, MO

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in O'Fallon, MO homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. Panel capacity gets checked up front.
