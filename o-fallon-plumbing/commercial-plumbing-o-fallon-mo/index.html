    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in O'Fallon, MO

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. A flushometer that runs, a urinal that will not stop, or a sensor faucet that drips are usually fixed with a diaphragm kit, a solenoid or a new battery pack. We stock those. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in O'Fallon

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. Trap primers get checked too.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in O'Fallon, MO

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. First we run a camera and locate the damage depth, then we compare a spot dig, a cured-in-place liner and pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Lining a 6-inch lateral can often be done in a day without tearing up asphalt or closing an entrance.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in O'Fallon, MO

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. We repair and replace sensor faucets, metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and three-compartment sinks, and we stock parts for Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass. Low-flow aerators cut water use without slowing hand washing. A typical fixture repair takes an hour or two.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in O'Fallon, MO

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. We service AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial units, gas and electric, tank and tankless, and we check thermostats, gas valves, flue draft and T and P valves on every call. For a replacement, we size from fixture count and peak demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in O'Fallon

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Expansive clay soils are common in Missouri, and they swell and shrink with moisture, putting stress on foundations and buried lines. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in O'Fallon

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. The camera goes in at a cleanout, we note distances to every defect and then locate each one from the surface with a receiver. The video is yours to keep. That record makes capital planning far easier when the board asks why the sewer needs money.

    Grease Trap Installation in O'Fallon

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.
