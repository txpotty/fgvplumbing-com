    How We Set New Sinks in St. Joseph, MO

    Drop-In Sink Installation in St. Joseph, MO

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in St. Joseph, MO

    New sinks often get a new disposal. We mount the disposal on a fresh flange with putty, connect the dishwasher knockout so the hose drains through it, and confirm the outlet sits above the trap inlet so it drains properly. An unpunched knockout backs up the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Commercial Sink Installation in St. Joseph, MO

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. Three-compartment sinks usually drain indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water within easy reach of every prep station on the line. Plan review comments come first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in St. Joseph

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in St. Joseph, MO

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. We fill the bowl to the overflow, pull the stopper, and watch every nut under the cabinet at full flow with a flashlight and a dry paper towel. Paper towels catch the smallest drip. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in St. Joseph

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in St. Joseph, MO

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in St. Joseph, MO

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.
