    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in St. Joseph, MO

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in St. Joseph, MO

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Settled low spots get extra passes, and we mark them on the video for future reference.

    Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in St. Joseph, MO

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in St. Joseph

    Slow drains in several rooms usually mean buildup in a main or branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Plan on two to three hours.
