    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in St. Joseph

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.
