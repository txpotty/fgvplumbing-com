    Underground Pipe Repair in St. Joseph

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. You rarely see them. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, and either spot repair at the leak or replace the line by pulling new pipe through. We test before backfill.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in St. Joseph

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. One usually means more are coming.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in St. Joseph

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, so we swap the failing pipe for PEX or copper with proper fittings between metals. You will notice the flow.

    Buried Line Repair in St. Joseph

    Buried pipes can be damaged by settling, roots or deep frost. Disconnecting hoses, insulating exposed lines and letting faucets drip on bitter nights head off most freeze damage.     Burst Pipe Repair in St. Joseph
