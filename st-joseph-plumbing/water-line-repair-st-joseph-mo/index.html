    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in St. Joseph

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, which are worth replacing when other plumbing work is planned. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it.
