    Wall Waterproofing Work We Do in St. Joseph, MO

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in St. Joseph

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in St. Joseph

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in St. Joseph, MO

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Basement Wall Sealing in St. Joseph, MO

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Hot, humid summers and cold winters give Missouri plumbing a wide range of stress, with big temperature swings in between. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in St. Joseph, MO

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, which are best replaced when other plumbing work is planned. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in St. Joseph

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Hot, humid summers and cold winters give Missouri plumbing a wide range of stress, with big temperature swings in between. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Drainage System Integration in St. Joseph, MO

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation.
