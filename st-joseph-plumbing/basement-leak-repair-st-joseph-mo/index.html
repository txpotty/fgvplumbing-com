    Waterproofing for Older Missouri Homes in St. Joseph, MO

    Older basements usually went in with block walls and little more than tar on the outside.     Sump Pump Repair and Installation in St. Joseph, MO

    Plenty of basements rely on a sump pit to handle runoff and groundwater. Sudden downpours often overload older sewer systems, which can push water back up through basement floor drains.     Clogged Drain Tile Repair in St. Joseph

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. Jetting clears it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Wall Leak Solutions in St. Joseph, MO

    Damp walls without a crack usually mean water moving through porous concrete or block. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Real seepage calls for better drainage or an interior channel.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes rust through from the inside over decades. A wet spot at the base of the stack or a crack along the hub is common, and we replace the damaged section with PVC or new cast iron joined with no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. Loose PVC joints usually trace back to poor support or a fitting that was never fully seated.
