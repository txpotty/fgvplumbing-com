    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in St. Joseph, MO

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper tends to pinhole. Old copper wears through where it touches gravel or reacts with the water, while PEX usually leaks at a fitting that was crimped short or chewed by rodents near an access point. Trucks in St. Joseph, MO carry parts for both.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in St. Joseph

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead.
