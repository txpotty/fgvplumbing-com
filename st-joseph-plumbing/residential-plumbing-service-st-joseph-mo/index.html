    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in St. Joseph

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Basements are common in many areas, so sump pumps and drain tile are an important part of keeping homes dry. We locate them with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute through the attic.

    Repiping and Supply Line Replacement in St. Joseph

    Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning. We replace it, along with corroded galvanized and pinholed copper, using PEX or copper, routing new lines through the attic and walls so the slab stays intact and the house keeps water each night. We patch the access holes, ready for paint.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in St. Joseph, MO

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early.
