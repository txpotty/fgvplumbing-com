    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. The common rule is to pump when grease and solids fill a quarter of the liquid depth. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. Busy fryers fill a trap fast.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Hydrogen sulfide from the waste eats the concrete above the waterline over time. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Nobody goes in without a gas monitor, a tripod and a second person topside.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in St. Joseph, MO

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. We put both options in writing with a price on each.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in St. Joseph

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. On old cast iron we lower the pressure and watch the camera so we do not open up a thin spot.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. Once cracked, it leaks smell out and lets mop water into the trap. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in St. Joseph

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. A cracked or loose baffle turns the trap into a pass-through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in St. Joseph

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. It sets up like candle wax on the crown of the line. Chemical drain openers soften it for a day and then it re-forms farther downstream, sometimes past the trap entirely, where it becomes a sewer problem instead of a kitchen problem. A jetter actually takes it out of the line.
