    Storm Drain Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. A working sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any home with a finished basement. Debris and silt fill them up. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and replace damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water flows to the outlet instead of pooling against the house. A clean outlet matters too.

    Restaurant and Office Drain Repair in St. Joseph

    Restaurants, salons, offices and medical suites all need working drains during business hours. Kitchens clog the most. We repair grease-laden kitchen lines, floor sinks, mop sink drains and restroom fixtures, working before opening or after close to keep the doors open. Maintenance plans catch trouble early.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. No drain clogs more often. We clear the branch, replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and correct trap heights so the arm has the right fall to the wall. The disposal and dishwasher hookups get a look too.

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. The repair gets a flow test.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Drain seals wear out. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, and failed drain gaskets can usually be replaced from above without cutting into the ceiling below. Any access hole is kept small and neat.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in St. Joseph, MO

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. Fine soil plugs it over time. Basements are common in many areas, so sump pumps and drain tile are an important part of keeping homes dry. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. We confirm it drains to daylight or a sump.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in St. Joseph

    Downspout lines and yard drains fail without much warning. Water pools at the house. We clear the line, camera it for crushed or disconnected sections, and replace the damaged pipe so roof water reaches the outlet instead of soaking the soil next to your foundation. We check the grade at the outlet too.

    Bathroom Drain Repair in St. Joseph

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Scale speeds it up. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, and fix leaking tub shoes and shower drain bodies that let water drip into the ceiling below. Most of these are one-hour jobs.
