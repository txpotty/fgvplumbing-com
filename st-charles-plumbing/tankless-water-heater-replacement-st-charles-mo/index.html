    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in St. Charles, MO

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. You see both quotes.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in St. Charles

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Most take under two hours.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in St. Charles

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Water heaters face heavy demand in winter, when incoming water is cold and households use more hot water. Showers and laundry run all year, so the gas savings do too.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in St. Charles

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Plan on four to six hours.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in St. Charles, MO

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. Both readings get taken.
