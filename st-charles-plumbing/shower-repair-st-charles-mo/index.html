    Shower Handle Leak Repair in St. Charles

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in St. Charles, MO

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in St. Charles

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Shut the main valve, catch the drip in a bucket and keep people out from under any sagging drywall, since a soaked ceiling can let go without warning. Then call us. We find the source, stop it and dry out what we can that day.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in St. Charles, MO

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.
