    What Our Repipe Crews Do in St. Charles, MO

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in St. Charles

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Commercial Repipe Services in St. Charles

    Older commercial buildings in St. Charles often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. We replace supply risers, branch lines and drains while keeping offices, shops or apartments running as much as possible. Shutoffs get scheduled ahead. We plan the work by zone and handle keys, access and quiet hours with your manager.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in St. Charles, MO

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. We replace it with PEX or copper. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in St. Charles

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in St. Charles

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. First we stop the leak and get water back on with a temporary fix, then we schedule the repipe without pressure. That gives you time. Your policy may pay for water damage even if not for the new pipe, so we take photos of everything we find.

    Residential Repiping Plans in St. Charles

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. Our estimator counts fixtures, checks access, finds the main and asks about any planned remodels before choosing a route. Then we size the lines. A three-bath house with a tankless heater may need 1-inch main trunk lines to keep showers steady when the washer kicks on.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in St. Charles, MO

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in St. Charles, MO

Do the math. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.
