    Resin Rebedding in St. Charles

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we drain the tank, pull the old resin and refill it with fresh high-capacity resin, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in St. Charles, MO

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold.     Breaking Up Salt Bridges in St. Charles

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. A hard stop, then a drop, means a bridge.
