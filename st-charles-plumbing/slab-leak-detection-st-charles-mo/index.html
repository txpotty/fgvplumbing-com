    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in St. Charles

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it.     Tracer Gas Leak Detection in St. Charles

    PEX and CPVC do not carry sound well.
