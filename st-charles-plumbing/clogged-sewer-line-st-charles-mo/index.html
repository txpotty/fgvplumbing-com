    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in St. Charles, MO

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Broken pipe needs repair. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, and the camera tells you which path makes sense. We walk you through it.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in St. Charles, MO

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. Starting in the right place matters.

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. The results hold up far better.

    Expansive clay soils are common in Missouri, and they swell and shrink with moisture, putting stress on foundations and buried lines. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly.
