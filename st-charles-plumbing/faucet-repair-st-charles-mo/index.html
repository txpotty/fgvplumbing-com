    Everyday Faucet Repairs in St. Charles, MO

    Ball Faucet Repair in St. Charles, MO

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in St. Charles, MO

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Look at the floor of the sink cabinet. New O-rings go on and the mounting nut gets tightened, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in St. Charles

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in St. Charles, MO

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. We measure the pipe stub and bring the right style, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in St. Charles, MO

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in St. Charles

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in St. Charles

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise. If they will not turn, use the main shutoff for the house. We come fast for these.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in St. Charles, MO

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. We carry commercial cartridges. Metering faucets get timed so they run long enough to rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.
