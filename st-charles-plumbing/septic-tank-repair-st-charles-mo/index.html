    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in St. Charles, MO

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A tilted or crushed D-box can send everything to one trench and drown it.
