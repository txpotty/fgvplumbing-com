    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Kansas City

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. That is what makes the popping noise. For stubborn scale we use a potable-rated descaler, then flush it clean. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.

    Rental and Commercial Tank Logs in Kansas City

    Landlords and property managers with a dozen units need to know which tank is next to fail. We log every tank we service. The log shows the serial date, anode condition, the last relief valve test and a rough estimate of years left, so you can plan replacements during a vacancy instead of on a Saturday night. We service 30 to 100 gallon tanks in rentals.

    Emergency Tank Inspections in Kansas City

    Sudden changes call for a look the same day: a tank that suddenly rumbles, hot water that turns brown, a puddle forming under the pan, or a relief valve that will not stop dripping. Diagnosis comes first. Sometimes the fix is a flush and a new drain valve, and sometimes the tank is leaking from the liner and nothing will save it.

    Commercial Tank Maintenance in Kansas City, MO

    Restaurants, salons, laundromats and small offices run tanks much harder than a household does, often 75 to 100 gallons cycling all day. Scale piles up much faster than at home. Most commercial tanks go on a quarterly or twice-yearly schedule with a dated log on the jacket. Downtime gets scheduled outside business hours.

    Electric Element Check in Kansas City

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. We test each one with a meter. A failed lower element shows up as hot water that runs out halfway through a shower. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Kansas City, MO

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and appliances. We break the hex head loose, pull the rod and check how much bare wire shows. Six inches of bare wire means replace it.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Kansas City

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Kansas City

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. We match the new valve to the heater's input rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. Never plug or cap that pipe.
