    Rooting vs. Pipe Repair in Kansas City, MO

    Rooting makes sense when the pipe is sound and just clogged. If roots come back within a few months, or the cable keeps catching on a broken hub, a repair starts to pay for itself. The camera settles it. Rooting usually runs a few hundred dollars, and lining or replacing a section costs more but ends the repeat calls.
