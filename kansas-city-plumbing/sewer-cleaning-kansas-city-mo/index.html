    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Kansas City, MO

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. One pipe, many fixtures. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Kansas City

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Root Masses in Kansas City Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Clay joints let roots in. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Kansas City, MO

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Groundwater from limestone areas is especially mineral-heavy, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. Flow slows a little each year. A cable can punch a hole through the crust, but a jetter cleans the walls back to full size and leaves less for new grease to grab onto, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Cleaning in Kansas City

    Sewage coming up through a shower or floor drain? Shut off the washer and dishwasher. A tech arrives ready to clear the main and start cleanup, with the goal of restoring flow before anything else, then we disinfect the area and take photos for an insurance claim.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Kansas City, MO

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Some older neighborhoods still have lead service lines, which are worth replacing when other plumbing work is planned. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Kansas City, MO

    Some lines need a look first. Fragile pipe gets the camera before the cutter. Seeing the pipe first lets us pick the cheapest method that will work, and avoid damaging a line that is already failing.
