    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Kansas City, MO

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Kansas City

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Water Heater Inspections in Kansas City

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Kansas City

    Businesses in Kansas City use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns. We look at backflow assemblies, water heaters, restroom flush valves, floor drains and trap primers and the building sewer. Closures are expensive. With a ranked report, repairs get scheduled after close instead of forcing a shutdown.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Kansas City

    Groundwater from limestone areas is especially mineral-heavy, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Kansas City, MO

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. Backflow devices, grease traps, large heaters, flushometers and the main sewer are all part of it. Inspectors like documentation. Managers use the report to fix problems before an inspector or water provider flags them.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Kansas City, MO

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Kansas City, MO

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging.     Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Gurgling drains count too. Damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell under a sink or a warm patch of tile can all point to a hidden leak that an inspection will find before it does more damage.
