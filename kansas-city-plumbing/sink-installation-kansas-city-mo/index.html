    How We Set New Sinks in Kansas City, MO

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Kansas City

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. A same-size drop-in swap usually takes a morning, and a deeper bowl may only need a new trap. New counters add a day. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Kansas City, MO

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. We set the top in silicone, mount the faucet and drain first, and adjust the trap arm to meet the tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Kansas City, MO

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. An undermount on new stone costs more because of clips, cure time and a second trip to connect the plumbing, and a vessel sink adds a new drain and often a taller or wall-mounted faucet. Relocating the drain is the biggest add. We price each piece on the quote.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Kansas City

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Kansas City

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in Kansas City, MO

    New sinks often get a new disposal. We mount the disposal on a fresh flange with putty, connect the dishwasher knockout so the hose drains through it, and confirm the outlet sits above the trap inlet so it drains properly. An unpunched knockout backs up the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Kansas City, MO

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. We check the spec sheet first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Kansas City

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. We tie both drains into the wall with either two separate traps or a continuous waste to one trap, depending on the spacing, and add a new pair of stops for the second faucet. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.
