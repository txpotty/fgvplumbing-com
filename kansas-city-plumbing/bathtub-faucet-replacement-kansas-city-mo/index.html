    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Kansas City

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Kansas City, MO

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Kansas City

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. Swapping a standard spout for a long-reach filler or a handheld shower on a slide bar changes how the tub works, and both usually need a new valve or a diverter added inside the wall. We plan the rough-in first. The trim goes on last.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Kansas City, MO

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. We stock common Delta cartridges. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Kansas City

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Kansas City

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Kansas City, MO

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. Snapped handles and broken spouts get same-day visits when a plumber is free. Close the stops or main while you wait. Leave the tub drain open.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Kansas City

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.
