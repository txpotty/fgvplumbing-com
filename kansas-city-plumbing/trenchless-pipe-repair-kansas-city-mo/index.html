    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Kansas City

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Kansas City

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Kansas City yard.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Kansas City, MO

    Per foot of pipe, trenchless can cost more than open trenching. Look at the full cost. After adding drive, patio, landscaping and sprinkler repairs to the trench bid, trenchless usually wins on price and speed. We compare both with real numbers for your home.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Kansas City

    Older Kansas City homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Kansas City, MO

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.

    Same-Day Sewer Assessment in Kansas City, MO

    Sewer lines that fail suddenly can often be cleared and scoped the same day. Lining comes next. When lining fits, the repair gets booked fast, and until then we keep it flowing and tell you what not to send down the drain.

    Pipe Bursting in Kansas City, MO

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Kansas City, MO

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes. FGV Plumbing joins the new pipe by heat fusion.
