    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Kansas City

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Kansas City, MO

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Kansas City

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. We respond quickly, locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test the utility needs to see before it will restore service. Most are one-visit repairs. We stay until the system holds its test pressure and the meter is ready to be unlocked.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Kansas City

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. This is a quick fix. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Kansas City

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. An electronic detector does the rest. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.
