    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Kansas City

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. A cut cable can snap hard. Before we open the floor, we scan with ground-penetrating radar to map the cables, and if the leak sits too close to a tendon, rerouting the line is the safer call.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Kansas City, MO

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It's usually the lowest bid.     PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Kansas City

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. It still leaks. Usually the culprit is a fitting that was not crimped all the way or a glued joint that separated when the ground shifted.
