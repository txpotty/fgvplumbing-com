    Well Pump Brands We Install in Kansas City, MO

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Parts for Goulds and Franklin are easy to get, which matters when a control box dies on a weekend.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Kansas City

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Sand in the impellers and lightning surges are the usual culprits. We megger the motor before we pull it. The reading shows whether the fault is the motor or the wire.

    Well Pump Sizing in Kansas City

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Kansas City, MO

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. Add the CU 301 controller to an SQE and you get constant pressure.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Kansas City, MO

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. We pressure test the line. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.

    Emergency Well Pump Service in Kansas City, MO

    Lost water at the house? Call (816) 330-2338 and tell us what the pressure gauge reads. Zero with the pump running points one way, zero with the pump silent points another, and that tells us which parts to load. Same-day water is the goal.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Kansas City, MO

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. Those get tested first. A $60 part beats a $2,500 pump pull.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Kansas City

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. Timing matters on a ranch.
