    Waterless Urinal Installation in Kansas City

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Kansas City

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Models like the Washbrook and the Bardon come in versions from 0.125 to 1.0 gallons per flush, and each one needs a flush valve matched to that rating or the bowl will either splash or not rinse clean. Matching is simple with the spec sheet. We order both together.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Kansas City, MO

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. A low-flow bowl and matching valve on the existing carrier cut water use sharply. Ask your water utility about rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Kansas City, MO

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Sensor valves add a few hundred. We itemize each piece.

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Each brand uses its own cartridge, and in busy restrooms it may need changing every few months. A replacement schedule comes with the install. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Kansas City, MO

A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.
