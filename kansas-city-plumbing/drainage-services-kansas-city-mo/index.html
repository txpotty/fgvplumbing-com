    French Drain Installation in Kansas City

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. Filter fabric around the stone keeps silt out. It needs steady fall to an outlet, and the outlet gets a grate to keep critters out.

    Commercial Drainage Solutions in Kansas City

    Businesses in Kansas City lose real money when a floor drain backs up. Floor drains, grease lines and lot catch basins get serviced before or after business hours. Scheduled jetting stops most emergency calls.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Kansas City, MO

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send runoff straight into a garage or a door threshold. A channel drain catches that sheet flow. We saw-cut the slab, set a polymer or concrete channel with a grate rated for the traffic it will see, and pipe it in solid PVC to a safe discharge point away from the foundation.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Kansas City

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Cables punch through clogs, but jetting removes the grease and scale coating the pipe, which is what keeps causing them. Restaurants need it most. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough to jet.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Kansas City, MO

    Recurring drain trouble needs a camera to see what is going on. We push a waterproof camera through the cleanout and record the trip to the street, and a locator in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth of any problem on your lawn. You get the video. It is a smart step before buying a house.
