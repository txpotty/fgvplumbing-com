    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Kansas City

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. Each part gets tested, the burner gets cleaned and the vent gets a draft check.     Electric Water Heater Repair in Kansas City

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. If the water is warm but runs out quickly, the lower element is the first suspect. We test resistance with a meter. Elements and thermostats are on the truck, so the fix is usually done the same visit.

    Water Heater Leak Repair in Kansas City, MO

    Water on the floor next to the heater needs a closer look before anyone panics. A drip from the relief valve or a supply fitting is usually a modest repair. Tank body leaks are different, because a failed inner lining cannot be patched. A drain pan helps on a slab.

    Electric Tank Repair in Kansas City, MO
