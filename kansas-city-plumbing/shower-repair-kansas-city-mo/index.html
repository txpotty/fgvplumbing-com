    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Kansas City

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Kansas City, MO

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Commercial Shower Service in Kansas City

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Kansas City, MO

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Kansas City

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. The drain gets cleared and tested. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Kansas City
