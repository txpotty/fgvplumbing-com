    What Goes Into a Tankless Install in Kansas City, MO

    Residential Tankless System Upgrades in Kansas City

    Upgrading from a tank in Kansas City starts with a look at what you have now: tank size, vent type, gas line size and where the unit sits. In some homes the old flue gets capped and the new unit vents out a side wall in PVC, which is simpler and opens the old chase. Some homes need more vent pipe. We spell that out on the quote.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kansas City

    Sizing a home unit comes down to two numbers: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. With a shower at about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen tap a bit under, two showers and a sink together need around 5 or 6 gallons per minute at the needed rise. That narrows the model list fast.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Kansas City

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kansas City

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. That pump can push hot water toward a far bathroom on a schedule, which shortens the wait at the tap without adding a separate pump under the sink. Venting is usually two-inch or three-inch PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Kansas City, MO

    Businesses in Kansas City, MO with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. If one unit goes down, the others keep running. We size the rack by peak demand. Expect a bigger gas line and a mechanical permit on commercial jobs.

    Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in Kansas City, MO

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem in Kansas City, MO homes every week. Rinnai has a wide model range and good parts support, Navien builds in recirculation on many models, Noritz makes compact condensing units that fit tight spaces, and Rheem pairs well with their tank lineup. Each warranty differs. We walk you through them.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kansas City

    Gas tankless units in Kansas City are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We test the new gas piping before first fire.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Kansas City, MO

    Upfront, a tankless install in Kansas City, MO costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. The gap shrinks over time. Less standby loss, a life of up to 20 years versus roughly 10 for a tank, and replaceable parts all count over the years. Ask for both prices.
