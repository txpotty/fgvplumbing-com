    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Kansas City

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. That pipe is worn out. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, improves pressure, clears up discolored water, and removes the leak risk from aging threads. Worth it.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Kansas City, MO

    Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated garages are the most common trouble spots when temperatures drop sharply. Ice inside a pipe cracks it open. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, so we fix it and protect it from the next freeze. Then we test it.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Kansas City, MO

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Close the main shutoff right away. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and we can usually get there, stop the leak and replace the failed section the same day. Drying comes next.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Kansas City

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and appliances. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Kansas City

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. We locate the leak first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, and rerouting often costs less and avoids future slab leaks on that line. We explain both.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Kansas City

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Jackhammering floors is a last resort. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, which leaves the floors alone and prevents the same line from failing again. Fast too.

    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Kansas City

    Apartments and condos share risers. Shared lines mean shared problems. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, keeping each unit informed. Organized work.
