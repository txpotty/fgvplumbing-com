    What Goes Into an Ejector Install in Kansas City, MO

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kansas City, MO

    Businesses in Kansas City, MO with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Downtime costs money. Each pump can carry the load alone.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Kansas City, MO

    The basin holds everything, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. A larger basin means longer, less frequent cycles that spare the motor and switch, so we go bigger when the pit takes a laundry or several fixtures. Every lid gets sealed.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Kansas City, MO

    Upgrading an old ejector does not always mean a new basin. If the basin is sound, we can swap in a better pump, replace the old swing check with a new one, add a full-port shutoff and install a high water alarm, which covers most of what a new system offers for a fraction of the cost. We tell you if the basin is done.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kansas City

    Zoeller's sewage line runs from light residential to heavy duty, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. We pick the model by horsepower and pump curve for your lift, and a typical basement bathroom lands on a four-tenths or half horsepower unit with two-inch solids handling. Bigger lifts need more.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Kansas City

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. The slab gets repoured.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kansas City, MO

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Kansas City, MO

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Parts are easy to find.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Kansas City

    Ejector pumps usually last 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Signs of trouble include a pump that runs constantly, a grinding or humming noise, an alarm that keeps tripping and sewage odor near the pit, and any of those means it is time to call before the basement floor tells you. The float usually goes first.
