    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Kansas City

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. We drill the counter or use the spare faucet hole.

    Badger Series Installation in Kansas City, MO

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. All of them use the same quick-lock mount. That shared mount is why a Badger-to-Badger swap is often a 45-minute job, since the upper ring and flange can stay put if they are not corroded. No cord comes in the box, so the cord kit is on us.

    Badger Replacement for Kansas City Homes

    Builder-grade kitchens almost always started with a Badger, and a worn one usually gets replaced with another. We pull the old unit first. If the upper mount is the same quick-lock design and is not pitted, we reuse it, which saves time and keeps the seal surfaces that already match your sink. Then the new Badger hangs, locks and gets tested.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Kansas City, MO

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Kansas City

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Evolution Disposal Upgrades in Kansas City, MO

    Stringy food is where these units earn their price. Corn husks and celery will stall a light disposal. A Compact or Excel grinds in stages with more torque, so fibrous scraps get cut down instead of binding the impellers and tripping the reset. We check cabinet clearance first.

    Septic-Friendly Disposal Installs in Kansas City, MO

    Homes on septic can run a disposal, but food solids add load to the tank. Regular pumping and inspection keep septic systems working properly, and well water should be tested on a regular schedule. The Evolution Septic Assist injects an enzyme solution into the grind chamber each time it runs, which helps break down food waste before it reaches the tank. The enzyme cartridge gets mounted, and we show you the refill. Scraping plates into the trash still helps.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Kansas City, MO

    A new sink changes the drain math. Undermount and farmhouse bowls are often deeper than the old drop-in. With the bowl lower, the disposal drops too, and the discharge can land below the old trap arm, which means lowering the stub. We plan that during the remodel, not after.
