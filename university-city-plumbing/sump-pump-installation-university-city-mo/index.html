    Storm-Ready Sump Pump Installation in University City, MO

    Liberty Pumps Installation in University City, MO

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Replacement switches are easy to get. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in University City

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in University City, MO

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. Backup and combo units are available too. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in University City, MO

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    New Sump Pit Installation in University City

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. Then we patch the slab. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in University City

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in University City

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. Downtime costs money. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in University City, MO

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Access is often tight. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.
