    What Goes Into a Sewer Line Replacement in University City, MO

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in University City

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. Sewer gas and moisture eat away the bottom of the pipe first, so the camera often shows a channel worn through along the invert even when the top still looks solid. Scale snags paper and grease. Outside the foundation we replace it with PVC or HDPE. Under-slab cast iron gets a separate look at lining or rerouting.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in University City

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Where we can, the sod is cut and set aside before the digging starts. The inspector sees open pipe. Backfill goes in compacted lifts so the trench line does not sink into a ditch a year later.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in University City, MO

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. Most bursts finish in a day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in University City

    Restaurants and strip centers cannot close for a week. Commercial jobs get scheduled around your hours, with the equipment staged early and the work often done overnight. Bigger pipe, usually six inch. Grease and heavy flow change how we pick the material and set the cleanouts.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in University City

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line first, often with a cutter and a jetter, then pull a resin-saturated felt tube into place and inflate it so it cures hard against the host pipe. It needs a pipe that still holds its shape. It is a strong choice under slabs and finished rooms. We say plainly whether your line qualifies.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in University City, MO

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber.
