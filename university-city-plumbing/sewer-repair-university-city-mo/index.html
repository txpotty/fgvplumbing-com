    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in University City

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in University City

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in University City

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in University City

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, which keeps the dig to one small pit. Older homes in University City often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in University City, MO

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Basements are common in many areas, so sump pumps and drain tile are an important part of keeping homes dry. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.
