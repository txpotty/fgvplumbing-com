    What a Well Pump Job Costs in University City, MO

    A well pump job usually runs a few thousand dollars, and the pump itself is often less than half of that. Labor and hoist time, along with new wire and pipe, make up the rest. Every line is on the quote.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in University City

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. We check the air charge with a gauge. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in University City, MO

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. Showers stop going weak.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in University City

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. On the way up, we look at the torque arrestor and the splices. Most jobs are done in a day.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in University City, MO

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. We test them first. A $60 part beats a $2,500 pump pull.
