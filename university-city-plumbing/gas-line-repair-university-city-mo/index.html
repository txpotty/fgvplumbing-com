    CSST Repair in University City

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. Bonding gets checked on every system. The system should be bonded to the electrical grounding system with a proper clamp and conductor, per the manufacturer's instructions and local code.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in University City, MO

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. Each new appliance gets a properly sized branch, a shutoff you can reach, a drip leg where required and a new coated connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in University City

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Expansive clay soils are common in Missouri, and they swell and shrink with moisture, putting stress on foundations and buried lines. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.
