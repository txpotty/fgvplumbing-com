    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in University City, MO

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. Venting changes too. Both are on the estimate.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in University City

    Propane homes in University City are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Since propane runs at different pressure from natural gas, the regulator, line sizing and gas valve must all match, and the supplier may need to swap the tank regulator. A small tank can run short.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in University City
