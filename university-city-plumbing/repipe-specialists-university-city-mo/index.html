    Repiping for Hard Water in University City, MO

    Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in University City, MO

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in University City

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step.
