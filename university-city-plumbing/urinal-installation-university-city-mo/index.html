    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in University City

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. The old flush valve comes off, the supply gets capped behind the wall, and the new waterless bowl goes on the same carrier with its cartridge housing tied into the existing drain. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in University City

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in University City

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Urinal Brands We Install in University City, MO

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. We recommend brands whose valves and cartridges are easy to get locally, so repairs never wait. Each of those brands is routine for us. Spec sheets come first.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in University City

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. A replacement schedule comes with the install. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in University City, MO

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.
