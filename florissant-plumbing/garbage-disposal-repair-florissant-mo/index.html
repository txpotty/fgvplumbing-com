    Preventive Care and Longevity in Florissant, MO

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap.     A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems.
