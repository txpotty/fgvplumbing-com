    Standard Tank Tune-Up in Florissant

    A standard tune-up starts with the fuel off and the cold supply closed, then a full drain through the bottom valve until the water runs clear. That alone takes 30 to 40 minutes. We check the heating elements or burner, read the thermostat setting and look at every fitting on top of the tank for green crust or drips. Any weeping at the dielectric nipples goes on the report.

    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in Florissant, MO

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. Gas units get a burner, thermocouple and vent check. Electric units get a meter test on both elements and a look at the wiring at each thermostat, where loose terminals can scorch and melt the insulation. Both get a flush.

    Emergency Tank Troubleshooting in Florissant

    No hot water at all is usually a pilot, a gas valve, a tripped high-limit or a dead element. Those are fixable. With a leak, we first check whether water comes from a top fitting or from under the jacket. Leaks from under the jacket mean the tank itself has failed.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Florissant

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. The new valve must match the tank's BTU or kilowatt rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. A capped discharge pipe is a serious hazard.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Florissant, MO

    Garage and attic tanks live through bigger temperature swings than closet units, which wears out plastic drain valves and wiring insulation faster. A pan under an attic tank is not optional. We confirm the pan drain line is open and runs to a safe spot. Gas tanks in garages need their ignition source at least 18 inches off the floor unless they are FVIR models.

    Sediment Flushing in Florissant, MO

    Sediment is mostly calcium carbonate and sand, and it settles in a layer that can reach several inches on a neglected 50-gallon tank. That layer insulates water from the heat. Our method is short bursts of line pressure that churn up the bottom. The drain hose runs to a floor drain or outside, never onto a lawn where hot water can burn the grass.

    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in Florissant

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and State make most of the tanks in local garages, and each has quirks a tech should know before opening a drain valve. On some models the anode sits under a plastic top cap. Bradford White often uses a combo anode in the hot outlet nipple. We carry the right sockets for each brand.

    Rumbling is the deeper cousin of popping, and it means a thick bed of sediment is shifting and boiling on the bottom of the tank each time the burner lights. The noise grows each year. On electric tanks the scale coats the lower element.
