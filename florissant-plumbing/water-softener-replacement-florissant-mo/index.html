    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. A tank water heater on hard water can lose a noticeable share of its efficiency as scale builds on the bottom, and tankless units scale up even faster. A working softener stops that.

    Softener Loop Installation in Florissant, MO

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Florissant

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. What worked for three people can run out of capacity in a few days with five. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Florissant, MO

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Florissant, MO

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Florissant, MO

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. A metered valve with an efficient brine setting can cut salt use by a third or more compared with an old timer unit, and that adds up over fifteen years. We compare brands on that basis.
