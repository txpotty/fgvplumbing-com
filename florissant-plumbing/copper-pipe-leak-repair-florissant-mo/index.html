    A repair clamp or epoxy can buy time in an emergency. It is not permanent. A plumber should cut out the damaged section and install proper fittings, particularly when the leak is somewhere you cannot see. Shut the water off first.

    Keep pressure in range. Test it yearly. Soften hard water, limit recirculation pump run time and get any green crust on fittings looked at early, while homes with older copper should have it checked every few years. Early repairs cost less.

    Keep water pressure under about 80 psi. A regulator helps. Treating hard or aggressive water, insulating pipe in cold spots and limiting how long a recirculation pump runs slow down the corrosion that thins copper. Find the main valve now.
