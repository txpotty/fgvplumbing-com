    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Florissant

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Florissant

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Florissant, MO

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Groundwater from limestone areas is especially hard, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Florissant

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Venting is usually two-inch or three-inch PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.
