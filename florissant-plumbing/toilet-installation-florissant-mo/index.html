    Toilet Installation Work We Handle in Florissant, MO

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Florissant

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Florissant

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Each one has its own parts quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Florissant

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Florissant

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A taller toilet and a grab bar go a long way. While the wall is open, we add 2 by 6 blocking between the studs at grab bar height, so the bar can later be screwed into solid wood instead of drywall anchors that pull loose. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Florissant

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Florissant

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar. We measure the existing drain before ordering, since moving a drain two inches to hit the ADA centerline means opening the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Florissant

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Florissant

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. The carrier holds the whole load, so its feet get anchored to slab or framing and it gets plumbed before any wall finish goes on. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Toilet Installers Near You in Florissant, MO

    Older Florissant homes can hide surprises under the toilet, like a cast iron flange rusted thin or a subfloor gone soft from an old leak. We check for both before setting anything. Any soft plywood gets cut back and replaced with new, glued and screwed down. It adds an hour, sometimes two.

    Toilet installers near you in Florissant should be licensed and insured, and they should be willing to pull a permit when the job calls for one. Ask before anyone sets foot inside. Moving a toilet or adding a new bathroom usually needs a permit and an inspection, while swapping a toilet in the same spot usually does not. We tell you which applies.
