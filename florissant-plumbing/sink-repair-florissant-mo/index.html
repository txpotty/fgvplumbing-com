    What We Fix Under the Sink in Florissant, MO

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Florissant

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. Older compression faucets need a washer and seat, and newer cartridge or disc faucets need the cartridge itself or its seals. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Florissant, MO

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Florissant, MO

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Florissant

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Hard freezes happen every winter in Missouri, and the northern part of the state usually sees longer cold spells than the south. Sun breaks down plastic traps and supply lines left outside, and yard dirt and grass clippings clog the drain faster than kitchen scraps ever do. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Florissant, MO

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Florissant

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Florissant

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Florissant, MO

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A burst supply line can dump gallons a minute, and we answer at night and on weekends with a stocked truck. Close the angle stops while you wait. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Local Sink Repair Near Me in Florissant, MO
