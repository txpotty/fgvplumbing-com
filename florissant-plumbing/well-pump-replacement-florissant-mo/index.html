    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Florissant, MO

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. We test them first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Florissant, MO

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Florissant, MO

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They are pricier than a standard pump. The SQE pairs with a CU 301 controller for constant pressure.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Florissant, MO

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Florissant

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. If the pump clicks on every time someone runs a faucet for ten seconds, the bladder in the tank has probably failed and the pump is short cycling itself to death. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Florissant

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Sand in the impellers and lightning surges are the usual culprits. We megger the motor before we pull it. The reading shows whether the fault is the motor or the wire.
