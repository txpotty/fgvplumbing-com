    Which Sump Pump Suits Your Florissant, MO Home

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Florissant

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. Most dead Wayne pumps we see have a cooked motor or a float tangled in the power cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Figure on a couple of hours.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Florissant, MO

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Florissant

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. It is a different pump entirely. The ejector and check valve get replaced, then we reseal the lid and check the vent connection. A bad seal shows up as sewer odor.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Florissant, MO

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. We move those calls to the front of the line. The tech brings a replacement pump and a new check valve and can have water moving out of the pit within an hour of arrival in most cases. Then we find out why it failed.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Florissant

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Florissant, MO

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Emergency Sump Pump Swaps in Florissant, MO

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (816) 330-2338 and we will send someone. Keep the lid off, unplug anything sitting on the wet floor, and use a wet vac to hold the water below the rim until we get there. Common pumps ride on the truck, so most swaps happen right then. We also check why the old one failed.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Florissant

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Sometimes a new switch is all you need, and sometimes the motor windings are shot. We tell you which before we sell you anything.
