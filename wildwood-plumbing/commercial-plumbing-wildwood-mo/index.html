    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Wildwood, MO

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. We install and service high-input tank heaters, tankless racks and boiler-fed indirect systems with storage tanks. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and fixtures. Scale cuts output fast. We put the heaters on a descaling schedule and install mixing valves so hand-wash sinks get tempered water at a safe temperature.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Wildwood, MO

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. We repair and replace sensor faucets, metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and three-compartment sinks, and we stock parts for Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass. We can also swap in lower-flow aerators that still rinse hands well. A typical fixture repair takes an hour or two.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Wildwood, MO

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless units get a descaling flush. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Wildwood

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. A sectional machine opens the line, and a jetter cleans grease off the walls of the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so it stays open longer. We also check trap primers.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Wildwood

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Wildwood, MO

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. We service AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial units, gas and electric, tank and tankless, and we check thermostats, gas valves, flue draft and T and P valves on every call. For a replacement, we size from fixture count and peak demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.
