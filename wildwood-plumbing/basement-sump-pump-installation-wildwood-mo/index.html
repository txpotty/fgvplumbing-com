    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. That makes them quieter than a pedestal, and in Wildwood, MO we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Wildwood

    No pit yet? First we locate the low point where water gathers, saw cut the slab, dig roughly 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a liner surrounded by washed gravel so water can enter while fine soil stays out. New concrete goes back flush and level.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Wildwood, MO

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. A working sump pump and a backwater valve are worthwhile protection for any home with a finished basement.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Wildwood
