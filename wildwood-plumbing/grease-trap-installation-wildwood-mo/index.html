    What Goes Into a Grease Trap Installation in Wildwood, MO

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Wildwood, MO

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. A trap 40 feet from the sink lets grease cool and stick in the pipe before it ever reaches the unit. Closer is better. For outdoor tanks we measure the hose run from where a pump truck can park.

    Grease Trap Replacement and Upgrades in Wildwood

    Plastic and steel traps both wear out, just in different ways. With steel, rust eats the seams and the welded baffles first. Plastic cracks. When we pull an old trap we cap the lines, haul it out, set the new unit on the same footprint where possible and reconnect with no-hub couplings so the next swap goes faster.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Wildwood

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. Before anything ships, we check that the tank and covers carry an H-20 traffic rating where trucks will roll over them. Most go in over two to four days. The inlet invert has to sit a couple of inches above the outlet, and we shoot both with a laser level before backfill.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Wildwood

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. We pipe the air intake the way the manufacturer and the code both require. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. A trap that sits out of level loses capacity, so we shim it flat before connecting anything.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Wildwood, MO

    Small cafes and coffee bars often get by with a 10 to 25 GPM hydromechanical trap tucked under the prep sink or beside it. The unit goes on the floor or in a shallow recess, and the flow control fitting goes upstream. Clearance matters most. We leave room to pull the lid and baffle without unhooking the sink, because a trap nobody can open is a trap nobody cleans.

    Zurn Grease Trap Installation in Wildwood

    Zurn's indoor grease interceptors cover the common sizes from small under-sink units up to 100 GPM, and we set enough of them to know where the gaskets give trouble. The lid gasket is the usual leak point after a few years. We keep spares on the truck. Most Zurn units come with a flow control fitting, and we mount it on the inlet side at the height the instructions call for.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Wildwood, MO

    Price follows size and location more than brand. The low end is a small indoor unit with easy access and a short pipe run. Outdoor tanks with digging and a permit sit at the top of the range. We quote after a site visit.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Wildwood

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. The purchase price is higher than a passive trap. They also need a dedicated electrical circuit, which we coordinate with your electrician before the unit arrives. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.
