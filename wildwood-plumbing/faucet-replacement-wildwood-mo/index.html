    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Replacement in Wildwood

    Plan on $200 to $600 for a typical faucet replacement in Wildwood, MO. A simple swap on working stops sits low, and new shutoffs or a seized faucet that needs cutting push it higher. High-end fixtures raise the total. The price is set before we begin.

    Ceramic disc cartridges matter more than the brand name. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Grohe all use them on most models and tend to hold up well for many years in mineral-heavy water. A softener also helps.

    The old faucet leaves with us. Hauling it off is included, and solid brass faucets get recycled as scrap. No cleanup on your end.

    Most swaps take one to three hours. Coring a new hole in a counter or cutting out a fused faucet can stretch the job toward three hours. We stay out of your way.

    Major brands warranty their faucets, often for life on cartridges. Our workmanship is guaranteed as well.
