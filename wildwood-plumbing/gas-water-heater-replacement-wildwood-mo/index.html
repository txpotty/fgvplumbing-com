    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Wildwood, MO

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. We help you pick.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Wildwood

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter. The venting changes too. Before quoting, we measure pipe runs and check the meter rating, which keeps the price from moving later.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Wildwood, MO

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. Those burners need more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Wildwood, MO

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.
