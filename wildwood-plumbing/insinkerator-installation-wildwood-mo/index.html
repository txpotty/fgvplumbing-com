    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber.     Badger Disposal Install in Wildwood

    Most replacement disposals in local kitchens are a Badger. These 1/3 to 3/4 HP units handle everyday scraps and fit nearly any standard sink base.     Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Wildwood

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. Each type gets wired its own way.

    New Construction Disposal Installs in Wildwood

    New builds and gut remodels get the disposal in two trips. During rough-in, the drain stub height gets set and the electrician leaves a switched outlet in the sink cabinet.     Hard Water and Disposals in Wildwood

    Groundwater from limestone areas is especially hard, which makes softeners and regular water heater flushing worthwhile. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. It also drains slower, because mineral crust narrows the tailpiece and the trap, so we look inside the old drain parts before we connect anything new to them. If they are crusted badly, we swap them while we are there.

    Evolution Upgrade Installs in Wildwood, MO

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot. Later they notice fewer drain clogs, since the staged grind breaks stringy food into finer pieces before it hits the trap. We check for vibration with the unit running.
