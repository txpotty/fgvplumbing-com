    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Wildwood

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Wildwood

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The blockage sits downstream of every fixture. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes. Older Missouri homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. You get the footage either way.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Wildwood

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Wildwood

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Wildwood, MO

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Lining costs more per foot. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often make it the cheaper total job. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. You get each option in writing with a number attached.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Wildwood

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. The real repair may be scheduled for the next morning. In the meantime, we flag which drains to avoid.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Wildwood, MO

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Limestone and rocky ground in the southern part of the state can make digging for sewer lines slower and more costly. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Wildwood

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.
