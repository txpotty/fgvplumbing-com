    Fixing Showers in Wildwood, MO

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Wildwood

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. We carry the common parts. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Wildwood, MO

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Wildwood, MO

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. We replace the spout or stem. It is a short job.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Wildwood

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. A new cartridge fixes it. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Wildwood, MO

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Wildwood

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. The drain gets cleared and tested. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Wildwood, MO

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. Both ride on our trucks. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Wildwood

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. The trim comes off first. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.
