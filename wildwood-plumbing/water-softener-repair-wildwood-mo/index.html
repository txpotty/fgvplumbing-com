    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Wildwood

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Wildwood, MO

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site. Seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol valves ride on the truck, so most rebuilds happen on the first visit.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Wildwood, MO

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. FGV Plumbing repairs these units in Wildwood, MO. The media wears out in a few years.

    Urgent Water Softener Repair in Wildwood, MO

    Major leaks and total system failure call for a fast response. FGV Plumbing handles urgent softener calls in Wildwood, MO ahead of scheduled maintenance. We bypass the unit so the house keeps water, then repair the valve, the tank or the fitting that failed and caused the leak. Bypass first.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Wildwood, MO

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Wildwood, MO

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Wildwood, MO

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Wildwood, MO

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. Sound tank and valve, repair wins. An obsolete valve with scarce parts tips the decision toward a new head or a new unit.
