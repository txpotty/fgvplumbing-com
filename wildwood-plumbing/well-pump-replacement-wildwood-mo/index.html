    What We Fix on Well Systems in Wildwood, MO

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Wildwood

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Most fail from sand chewing up the impellers or a lightning surge that cooks the windings. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. The reading shows whether the fault is the motor or the wire.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Wildwood

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. On the way up, we look at the torque arrestor and the splices. Most jobs are done in a day.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Wildwood, MO

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. Pressure switches with burned contacts or a clogged pickup tube can leave the pump sitting idle while the tank drains to zero. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. You get to see the old part.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Wildwood, MO

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. After that, we watch the pressure gauge cycle from cut-in to cut-out.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Wildwood, MO

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. The common failure is losing prime. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Wildwood

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. Timing matters on a ranch.

    What a Well Pump Job Costs in Wildwood, MO

    A well pump job usually runs a few thousand dollars, and the pump itself is often less than half of that. Labor and hoist time, along with new wire and pipe, make up the rest. Every line is on the quote.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Wildwood

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. Wire gets the same look. Cracked insulation and taped splices get cut out.
