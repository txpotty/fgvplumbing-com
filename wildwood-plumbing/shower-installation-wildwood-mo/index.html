    How We Install Showers in Wildwood, MO

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Wildwood, MO

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Digital Shower Systems in Wildwood, MO

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. The outlet is an electrician's job. We plumb the valve and outlets.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Wildwood, MO

    Budget showers can still be built right. A one-piece unit with a good valve is a fraction of the cost of tile, and set properly on mortar it holds up for a long time. The valve is where to spend. You get the full price before we start.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Wildwood

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Shower Pan Installation in Wildwood

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. Prefab pans get a level check. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Shower Stall Installation in Wildwood

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. Fiberglass and acrylic bases go down on a mortar bed so they cannot flex, and flex is the usual reason a base cracks early. The drain connects from below. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Wildwood, MO

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. When a pan is cracked or a valve is leaking into the framing, we pull the damaged shower, check the joists and subfloor and install a new unit as quickly as the framing allows. Dry framing first. Then the new shower goes in.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Wildwood

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Pressure varies by house. A gauge reading comes first.
