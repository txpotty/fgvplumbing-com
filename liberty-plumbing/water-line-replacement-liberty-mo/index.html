    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Liberty, MO

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper.     Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Liberty

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. Inside, the pipe rusts once the zinc is gone. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, and that shows up as weak pressure and orange water, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Liberty, MO

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated garages are the usual trouble spots when temperatures drop sharply. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.
