    Pinhole Leak Repair in Liberty

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, which we fix by replacing that length with new pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Liberty

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Ice splits the pipe. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Liberty

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Stains give it away. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet, and we locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow before closing up. Vents get checked too.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Liberty, MO

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. We cut CPVC carefully.

    Pipe Leak Detection in Liberty, MO

    A repair starts with finding the leak. Guessing wastes drywall. We use electronic listening devices, thermal cameras and moisture meters, shutting off zones one at a time until the leak has nowhere to hide. One cut, one fix.

    When water is pouring through a ceiling or spraying under a sink, you need help right away. Locate your shutoff.
