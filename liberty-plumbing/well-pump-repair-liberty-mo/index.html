    Pressure Tank Repair in Liberty

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. We check the precharge with the tank drained. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Liberty, MO

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. We lock out the power before testing anything. All wiring and splices meet Missouri electrical code before power goes back on.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Liberty

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Liberty, MO

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. FGV Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Liberty, MO

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. Fast cycling kills pumps young. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    No-Water Same-Day Repair in Liberty

    A dead pump leaves the house without toilets, showers or drinking water. FGV Plumbing answers emergency well pump calls throughout Liberty and nearby areas and puts them ahead of routine work. The technician checks power and the switch first. A lot of no-water calls end right there, with the pump never leaving the well.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Liberty

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up instead of pulling it, and they run quiet for years, but motors, impellers and wire splices still fail eventually. After pulling the pump, we test each part and replace only the failed one. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Liberty, MO well.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Liberty

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. FGV Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Liberty, MO well. Electronics fail less often than people think.
