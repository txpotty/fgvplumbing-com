    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Liberty

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Liberty homes. Expect more noise.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Liberty, MO

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Sudden downpours often overwhelm older sewer systems, which can push water back up through basement floor drains. A second pump goes into the same pit on its own float switch, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. The main pump keeps doing the daily work.

    Wayne Sump Pump Installation in Liberty, MO

    Wayne builds everything from basic utility pumps to combo units with a built-in backup.     Emergency Sump Pump Installation in Liberty, MO

    A dead pump with water rising in the pit is a same-day call. The plumber shows up with a replacement pump already loaded, pulls the old unit, checks whether the float or the check valve caused the failure, and gets water moving again in Liberty, MO. Keep a wet vac running until then.

    Sump Pit Installation in Liberty, MO

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. A small 15 inch pit makes the pump cycle every minute in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    Same-Day Sump Pump Installation in Liberty

    Same-day emergency installs cover pumps that quit in the middle of a storm. We remove standing water from the pit first, then set a new pump on a new check valve, plug it into a tested GFCI outlet and watch it run through several cycles before leaving your Liberty basement.
