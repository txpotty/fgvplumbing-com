    Gas work belongs with licensed professionals. A bad joint can cause a leak, a fire or carbon monoxide poisoning, and unpermitted gas work usually fails inspection, can void appliance warranties and can cause trouble with your insurance claim. Leave this one to a pro.

    Black iron and CSST cover most of our work. Rigid black iron is the pick for exposed runs, while CSST bends through finished walls with fewer joints and installs faster, so the choice usually comes down to where the pipe will run and how much wall you want opened. Either is code compliant.
