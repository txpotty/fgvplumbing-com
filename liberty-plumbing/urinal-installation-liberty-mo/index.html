    Sensor Urinal Installation in Liberty, MO

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. We install battery or hardwired units, set the sensing range so the valve does not fire when someone walks past, and turn on a sanitary flush cycle if the restroom sits empty for long stretches. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Liberty, MO

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Sensor valves add a few hundred. We itemize each piece.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Liberty

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. We set vitreous china units on floor-mounted carriers, connect them with new outlet gaskets and stainless bolts, and pair them with a Sloan or Zurn flush valve sized to the bowl. We check partition spacing too. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Liberty, MO

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Home Gym and Garage Urinals in Liberty, MO

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. The drain ties to the nearest stack, a vent or AAV goes in where allowed, and a compact bowl fits the space. Freeze protection matters in garages. Ask us about the layout.

    Urinal Brands We Install in Liberty, MO

    Brand choice is mostly about parts support. We recommend brands whose valves and cartridges are easy to get locally, so repairs never wait. We install all of them. Spec sheets come first.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Liberty

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. A replacement schedule comes with the install. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Liberty, MO

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. Replacing them with 0.5 or 0.125 gallon models on the existing carrier, and swapping the flush valve to match, cuts water use sharply in any restroom that sees regular traffic. Ask your water utility about rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.
