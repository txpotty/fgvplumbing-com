    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Liberty

    Water heater installation in Liberty, MO runs about $1,200 to $4,000 or more. Type, capacity and fuel set most of the price, and any gas line, venting or electrical upgrades add to it. Tankless costs more. We quote it in writing after a look.

    Doing it yourself means handling gas, 240-volt power and pressurized water at once. Mistakes can cause gas leaks, CO exposure or electrical fires, and a DIY install can void the warranty. We do not recommend it.

    Tank heaters usually last 10 to 15 years, and tankless units can go 20 or more. Water quality, how often it gets flushed and how hard the household uses it all change the real number you get out of a given tank. Flush it once a year.

    Age, repair cost and efficiency decide it. If the heater is over ten years old or the repair costs more than half of a new one, replacing it usually makes more sense. A leaking tank is always a replacement.

    Popping or rumbling, rust-colored water, leaks or an age past ten years are the common clues. Any one of them can mean replacement is near.

    Household size and peak demand decide the size. Most homes with two to four people use a 40 to 50 gallon tank, and larger households move up to 60 to 80 gallons. Tankless goes by flow rate.
