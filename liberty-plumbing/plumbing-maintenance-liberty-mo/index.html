    Backflow Preventer Testing in Liberty

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Yearly tests are often required, and our certified testers check each valve and file results. Failed devices get rebuilt. Weather protection gets checked too. Testing on time avoids fines.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Liberty

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections with leak detector solution, make sure each appliance shutoff valve turns and replace any old or kinked flexible connectors. Gas work is done to code.     Softener Cleaning and Testing in Liberty

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. Brine, injector, resin and regeneration all get checked, and we test the water at a tap. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Worn resin gets swapped.

    Sump Pump Testing in Liberty, MO

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Severe thunderstorms and heavy rain can cause flash flooding across Missouri, especially along rivers and in low-lying neighborhoods. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Battery backups get load tested.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Liberty

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Minerals settle on the tank bottom, trap heat and cause the popping you hear when it fires. Flushing clears it. Tankless units get a descaling flush through the service valves with a pump and descaler. Regular service helps keep the warranty valid.

    Softener Upkeep in Liberty, MO

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. We check the bypass and drain line. Then we test the treated water.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Liberty

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. We drain sediment, check the anode rod, inspect the burner or elements and confirm the relief valve opens and reseats, whether the unit is a standard tank or a tankless model on the wall. Most failures give warning. We spot the signs early.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Liberty, MO

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. Visible gas piping, connectors and shutoffs get checked, and old flex connectors get flagged. Pipe material matters too. Older pipe types get flagged with repipe choices.
