    What We Fix on Faucets in Liberty, MO

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Liberty

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Liberty

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. New washers go in and the seat gets dressed or replaced with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Liberty, MO

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. We bring the common ones. Filtered water faucets at the kitchen sink get new cartridges or check valves as needed.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Liberty, MO

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. Cracked or discontinued faucets are different. You get a repair price and a replacement price so the choice is yours.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Liberty, MO

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Water in much of Missouri is moderately hard to hard, so scale gradually builds in water heaters, faucets and appliances. We pull the aerator with a key and clean or replace it, then check the cartridge screens. Low flow everywhere is a different problem.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Liberty

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Close the angle stops under the sink. Should those stops be stuck, the house main shutoff is the backup. We come fast for these.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Liberty, MO

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard use their own cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

Cartridges get matched for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.
