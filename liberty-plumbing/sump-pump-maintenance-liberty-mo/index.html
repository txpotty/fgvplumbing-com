    Battery Backup System Testing in Liberty, MO

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Liberty

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. We pump the basin down and scoop out the sediment by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Liberty, MO

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm.     Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Liberty, MO

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Float Switch Checks in Liberty, MO

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Liberty

    Sudden downpours often overwhelm older sewer systems, which can push water back up through basement floor drains. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. That leaves time to replace a weak pump before the first big storm, not during it.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Liberty

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. Duplex systems and high-water alarms get tested on a fixed schedule.
