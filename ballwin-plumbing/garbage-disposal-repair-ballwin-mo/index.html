    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Ballwin, MO

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Emergency Disposal Repair in Ballwin, MO

    If water is reaching the floor, that comes first.     Leak Detection and Repair in Ballwin, MO

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Ballwin

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Ballwin

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A bad solenoid or tripped overload in the panel kills the unit as fully as a jam does, but it gets fixed far faster.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Ballwin, MO

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. A failed motor means a new unit.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Ballwin, MO

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard are behind most disposal odors. Homes around Ballwin on private wells should test for hardness and bacteria before picking a treatment system. Mineral scale traps the food. We clean the splash guard and chamber, check the drain for buildup, and replace a worn guard, which often clears up a smell that months of lemon peels never touched.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Ballwin

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most are simple fixes.
