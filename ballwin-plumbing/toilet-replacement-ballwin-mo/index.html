    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Ballwin, MO

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Ballwin, MO

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank.     Urgent Toilet Replacement in Ballwin, MO

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Ballwin, MO

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs.     Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Ballwin, MO

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.
