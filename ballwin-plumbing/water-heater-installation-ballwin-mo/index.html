    Expansion Tank Installation in Ballwin, MO

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Ballwin

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Ballwin

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Many communities use a version of the International or Uniform Plumbing Code, and plumber licensing is handled locally. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Ballwin

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. Our commercial work covers high-BTU tanks and multi-unit tankless racks, along with the larger gas lines, venting and code inspections that come with them. We schedule around your hours. Installs can happen after close.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Ballwin, MO

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Ballwin, MO

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Some qualify for tax credits. We help you decide if one fits.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Ballwin, MO

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Ballwin, MO
