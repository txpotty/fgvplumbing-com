    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Ballwin

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Upfront Pricing on Disposal Work in Ballwin, MO

    Quotes from us list the unit and the labor as separate lines, with parts like a new flange or cord kit broken out too. Nothing gets tacked on later.     Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Ballwin, MO

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Upfront Disposal Installation Pricing in Ballwin, MO

    Price depends mostly on two things: the unit you pick and how much needs to change under the sink. A like-for-like swap is the low end. A first-time install that needs a new flange and a new outlet costs more, because that is real labor and sometimes an electrician's visit too. You get the number before we start.

    Evolution Series Installation in Ballwin

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Clearance under the bowl matters here. We measure from the sink bottom to the trap arm before the install so the unit does not crowd the trap. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Ballwin

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. The hose clamps onto the inlet and runs through an air gap or a high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from siphoning back into the tub. Both appliances get run together.
