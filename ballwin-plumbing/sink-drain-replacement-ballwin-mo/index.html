    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Ballwin

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Most glass and stone vessel bowls have no overflow hole, so they need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, and a slotted drain in one of those bowls leaks out of the slots right into the counter hole. Mismatched drains are common. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Ballwin

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Over time that wears the thin plastic drain and trap on a utility sink until it cracks, and lint from the washer standpipe often builds up in the trap so badly that it never drains at full speed again. We fit a 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Ballwin

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain.
