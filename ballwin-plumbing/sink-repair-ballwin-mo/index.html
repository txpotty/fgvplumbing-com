    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Ballwin

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated garages are the usual trouble spots when temperatures drop sharply.     Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Ballwin

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think.     Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Ballwin

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount bowls hang from clips epoxied to the stone, and pedestal sinks rely on a wall bracket that may have pulled out of old drywall over the years. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Ballwin, MO

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. Close the angle stops while you wait. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Ballwin

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.
