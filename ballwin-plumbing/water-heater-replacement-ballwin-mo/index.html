    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Ballwin, MO

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Ballwin

    Electric water heaters in Ballwin usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.
