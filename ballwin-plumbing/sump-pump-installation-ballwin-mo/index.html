    New Sump Pit Installation in Ballwin

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Ballwin, MO

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Clearance is usually under two feet. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Ballwin

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Ballwin, MO

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Ballwin

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Ballwin, MO

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. Undersized pumps get upsized. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Ballwin, MO

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Replacement switches are easy to get. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Ballwin

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Ballwin

    Sump pump installation in Ballwin, MO runs about $800 to $2,500 for most homes. The pump type and whether we dig a new pit move the price most. A battery backup raises the total. We quote it in writing after a site visit.

    Three to five hours covers most standard jobs. Cutting a new pit or rerouting the electrical can turn it into an all-day job, especially when we hit rock or an old footing under the slab. You will know the timeline before we start.

    Most homeowners are better off hiring it out. An undersized pump, a backward check valve or a discharge that dumps against the foundation can cause the very flooding you were trying to stop. Safety matters here.

    Doing it yourself is possible but risky. Getting the pit depth, the check valve orientation, the discharge route and the outlet wiring right takes experience, and one mistake shows up during the next storm. We do it daily.

    Seven to ten years is typical. Cast iron pumps with mechanical switches tend to outlast plastic ones, and a pump that rests longer between cycles lasts longer. Cleaning the pit helps.
