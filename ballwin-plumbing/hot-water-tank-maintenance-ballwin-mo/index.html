    T&P Valve Replacement in Ballwin

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. We match the new valve to the heater's input rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. Never plug or cap that pipe.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Ballwin

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. The harder the water, the sooner it matters. We drain the tank completely, refill it partway, drain it again and repeat until the hose runs clear, which on a heavily scaled tank can take four or five cycles. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Rental and Commercial Tank Logs in Ballwin

    Landlords and property managers with a dozen units need to know which tank is next to fail. We log every tank we service. You get serial date, anode and valve notes and an estimate of years left for each unit. We service 30 to 100 gallon tanks in rentals.

    Burner and Pilot Service in Ballwin, MO

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. We pull the burner assembly and brush it clean. A tired thermocouple drops the pilot, so we test it too. The goal is a steady blue flame.

    Expansion Tank Service in Ballwin, MO

    A thermal expansion tank is the small steel tank on the cold line above the heater, and it gives heated water somewhere to go when a check valve or pressure reducer closes the system. Our tech checks the air charge with a tire gauge. The precharge needs to match static pressure in the house. A waterlogged expansion tank sounds solid when you knock on it, and it lets pressure spike every time the burner fires.

    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in Ballwin

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Ballwin, MO
