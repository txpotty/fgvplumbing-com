    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Ballwin, MO

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out.     Slab leak detection typically costs $150 to $450. The fix has its own price. A spot repair usually runs $1,500 to $4,000, while a reroute is often $1,800 to $5,000 based on the run length. Whole-house repipes are quoted by home size.

    Regular plumbing checks catch problems early. Watch your water pressure. Keeping house pressure under 80 psi with a working regulator, using good pipe in any repipe and calling when you notice a pressure change all reduce the chance of another slab leak. Ballwin homes are no different.
